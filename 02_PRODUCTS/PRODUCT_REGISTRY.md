# ClinicOps Product Registry

## Free entry products

### EUDAMED Identifier Check
Purpose: create trust and demonstrate regulatory intelligence.

### Transition Readiness Score
Purpose: identify potential customer needs.

## Paid products

### Transition Intelligence Scan
Input: device portfolio and evidence.
Output: risk map, priorities, recommended actions.

### Portfolio Intelligence Monitoring
Recurring service for regulatory signals and changes.

### Regulatory Operations Training
Structured learning products.

## Product rule

Each product must define:

- target customer
- problem solved
- evidence required
- delivery method
- success metric
- next improvement loop
