# ClinicOps Client Delivery Engine

## Purpose
Turn intelligence into repeatable client outcomes.

## Workflow

Lead
→ Qualification
→ Intake
→ Evidence collection
→ Analysis
→ Human validation
→ Report delivery
→ Feedback capture
→ Monitoring subscription

## Delivery products

### Transition Intelligence Scan

Output:
- executive summary
- device portfolio view
- evidence gaps
- priority actions
- recommended next steps

### Monitoring Service

Output:
- regulatory signals
- document changes
- transition updates
- periodic intelligence brief

## Quality gates

Before delivery:
- evidence checked
- limitations stated
- confidence recorded
- client data protected
