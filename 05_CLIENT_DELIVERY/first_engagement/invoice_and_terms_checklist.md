# Invoice and Terms Checklist — sole founder, EU B2B (VERIFY WITH AN ACCOUNTANT)

**This is a question list, not tax or legal advice. Every item must be confirmed with an accountant (and a lawyer for contract terms) in the founder's actual country of establishment.** The brief says Spain/Denmark-based: rules and forms differ, so the first decision is which country is the place of establishment and where the business is registered.

## 0. Decisions to make first
- [ ] Country of establishment/tax residence for the business; registered form (Denmark: enkeltmandsvirksomhed with CVR; Spain: autónomo registration). `[FOUNDER + ACCOUNTANT]`
- [ ] VAT registration active in that country and a VAT ID issued. Whether the intra-EU services need any extra registration/listing there.
- [ ] Business bank account and invoice currency (EUR/DKK). Currency conversion rule for the VAT return.
- [ ] Accounting/invoicing software that meets local bookkeeping and e-invoicing rules.

## 1. VAT (cross-border B2B services)
Common position to verify: services supplied to a business customer established in another EU member state are generally taxed where the customer is established, and the customer accounts for the VAT (reverse charge).
- [ ] Confirm this applies to ClinicOps services as described in the SOW (a review/analysis service, not a service tied to land or other special rules).
- [ ] Collect the customer's VAT ID before invoicing; validate in VIES; keep the validation record (date, result).
- [ ] Customer in the same country as the founder: normal domestic VAT applies. Customer outside the EU: different rules.
- [ ] Invoice must show: both VAT IDs, net amount, no VAT charged, the reverse-charge wording required locally (e.g. "Reverse charge" / national-language equivalent, with legal reference) `[ACCOUNTANT to give exact text]`.
- [ ] Periodic EU sales/recapitulative listing and how to declare reverse-charge sales in the VAT return (each country has its own form and deadline). `[ACCOUNTANT]`
- [ ] If the customer has no valid VAT ID (or is a private individual), treatment changes; do not invoice without deciding.

## 2. Contract
- [ ] SOW + pilot proposal accepted in writing (signature or accepted PO) before work starts and before any client data is received.
- [ ] Lawyer review of: liability cap and exclusions, personal liability of a sole trader and whether professional-indemnity insurance is needed, confidentiality, IP, governing law/venue, termination, data-processing terms.
- [ ] Standard terms and any client PO terms: which prevail. The pilot gate needs "standard commercial terms"; any client redlines are a founder decision, not silently accepted.
- [ ] Testimonial/case-study clause stays opt-in (`pilot_offer.md`).
- [ ] GDPR: privacy notice link, whether a DPA is needed, sub-processors, retention as in SOW.

## 3. Payment terms
- [ ] Fee, currency, VAT line `[TO BE SET BY FOUNDER]`.
- [ ] Deposit/milestones. Repo pilot gate needs one of: upfront payment, deposit/first milestone, accepted PO/signed commitment.
- [ ] Due date (e.g. `[N]` days from invoice date), bank details, payment reference, accepted methods, bank charges borne by whom.
- [ ] Late-payment interest and fixed recovery cost: EU late-payment rules exist but national implementation differs; state what applies `[ACCOUNTANT/LAWYER]`.
- [ ] Reminder routine and what happens to further work if unpaid.
- [ ] Customer procurement: PO numbers, supplier onboarding, e-invoicing portals (public-sector or large-company customers may require e-invoice formats).

## 4. Invoice content and records
- [ ] Sequential unique number; issue date; supply date/period; supplier and customer legal names, addresses, tax IDs; description of service matching SOW; net, VAT rate/none, total; currency and exchange rate if converted.
- [ ] Country-specific mandatory invoice elements and any electronic-invoicing or certified-software requirement (Spain and Denmark each have their own current rules; check timelines) `[ACCOUNTANT]`.
- [ ] Credit-note procedure; how a corrected invoice is issued.
- [ ] Retention period for invoices and accounting records in the country of establishment; keep the invoice separate from client data (client data is purged per SOW; the invoice is not client data).

## 5. Income tax and admin (raise with the accountant)
- [ ] Income recognition, estimated tax/social-security payments, deductible costs, home office, any special regime for new sole traders.
- [ ] Whether cross-border sales change social-security or permanent-establishment position (unlikely for remote services, but confirm).
