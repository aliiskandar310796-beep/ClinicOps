## What you get
One Regulatory Change Integrity Review (Type A) **or** one portfolio-scoped review (Type B) of `[N]` records/surfaces against a named authoritative source, with an evidence window ending `[date]`. Output: a source-linked, reviewer-approved exception queue (Source · Expected · Observed · Status · Evidence · Owner · Next Action · Closure), a report, a machine-readable file and a hash manifest, plus one review call. ## Success criteria (agreed before start; measured at closure)
Choose the ones that apply and set targets with the client. Targets left `[ ]` deliberately; the repo asserts no savings rate.
1. Coverage: `[ ]`% of declared surfaces processed or explicitly listed as unresolved.
2. Traceability: 100% of findings carry a source link, status, owner and next action.
3. Client-confirmed findings: at least `[ ]` findings confirmed by the client's qualified reviewer as real record/document gaps; false positives counted and reported.
4. Time: client manual minutes per surface before vs after, and ClinicOps review minutes, as recorded in `closure_record_template.md`.
5. Decision value: client states whether it would repeat the review on the next change (yes/no/why).
6. Delivered on time; all review-gate attestations complete.
Failure is reported plainly: if few or no findings, the report says so; no result is "adjusted" to look successful.
