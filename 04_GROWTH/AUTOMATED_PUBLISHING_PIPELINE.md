# ClinicOps Automated Publishing Pipeline

## Objective
Convert validated regulatory intelligence into repeatable market visibility.

## Pipeline

Signal
→ Evidence validation
→ Research note
→ Website article
→ LinkedIn draft
→ Newsletter
→ Lead magnet
→ Customer conversation

## Rules

- No publication without evidence source.
- Separate observations from interpretations.
- Regulatory claims require review.
- Every asset must have a target audience and business purpose.

## Metrics

Track:
- impressions
- qualified visitors
- downloads
- conversations started
- assessments requested
- revenue generated

## Improvement loop

Every published asset creates:
- market feedback
- keyword data
- customer questions
- product improvements
