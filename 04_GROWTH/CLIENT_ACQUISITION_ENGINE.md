# ClinicOps Client Acquisition Engine

## Objective

Turn verified regulatory or operational signals into qualified buyer conversations, bounded paid reviews, repeat work and referrals — without mass outreach, fear-based compliance claims or duplicate systems.

ClinicOps does not optimize for traffic, impressions or contact volume in isolation. The commercial loop is:

```text
verified trigger
→ affected account cohort
→ evidence-backed account hypothesis
→ relevant buyer
→ one useful proof object
→ qualified conversation
→ bounded paid review
→ accepted delivery
→ recurrence / referral
→ product + research learning
```

## Source of truth

- **GitHub:** public method, software, synthetic fixtures, governance, sanitized aggregate learnings.
- **HubSpot:** companies, contacts, deals, tasks, meetings, emails and E0–E9 commercial evidence.
- **Apollo:** account/contact discovery, research, enrichment and controlled outbound infrastructure.
- **Microsoft 365 / approved sender:** one-to-one business email and replies.
- **Google Search Console / search surfaces:** discoverability and query evidence.
- **ClinicOps website:** proof objects, browser-local tools and privacy-preserving scope intake.

Never place private prospect lists, buyer messages or confidential customer material in the public repository.

## Priority motions

### 1. Trigger-led manufacturer motion

Use current, source-linked regulatory or product-change signals to identify manufacturers likely to face reconciliation work.

Examples of useful trigger families:
- EUDAMED / UDI transition workload;
- SS(C)P lifecycle or translation workflow changes;
- certificate / portfolio transitions;
- product or regulatory changes affecting several controlled surfaces;
- observable portfolio complexity.

Do not infer non-compliance from a public-data gap.

### 2. Authorised-representative / consultancy channel motion

Prioritize ARs and regulatory consultancies where one relationship may expose repeated multi-manufacturer reconciliation work.

Test overflow, co-delivery and evidence-layer use cases before building a formal partner programme.

### 3. High-intent inbound motion

Route visitors from research, the Scanner, specimens and use-case pages into a browser-local scope brief.

Campaign parameters may be carried locally into the generated email draft only when the visitor explicitly creates it. No silent tracking.

## Account qualification

A target account is useful only when there is an explainable reason to contact it.

Score commercially relevant signals, not regulatory risk:

- regulatory trigger relevance;
- portfolio complexity;
- number of regulated surfaces;
- EU exposure;
- public evidence available for a grounded hypothesis;
- likely repetition of the workflow;
- reachable responsible role;
- partner multiplier;
- timing.

Do not use a numeric score publicly and do not label an organisation compliant or non-compliant.

## Message contract

Every outbound message must contain:

1. **Why this account** — one sourced, professional reason.
2. **One workflow problem** — not a service catalogue.
3. **One proof object** — Scanner, specimen, research note or bounded method page.
4. **One low-friction question** — useful to answer without accepting a meeting.
5. **Respectful exit** — no pressure and immediate suppression on objection.

No generic AI personalization.

## Volume control

Default to high-information, low-volume outreach.

Before any outbound:
- check HubSpot;
- check private suppression state;
- check prior sent/reply state;
- verify jurisdiction / route suitability;
- verify the message hypothesis and source;
- keep the current company-wide send ceiling.

Do not activate automated sequences merely because there was no reply.

## Commercial evidence ladder

- E0 — hypothesis only
- E1 — public market / workflow signal
- E2 — qualified reachable account
- E3 — outreach delivered
- E4 — substantive human response / qualified conversation
- E5 — repeated pain and consequence confirmed
- E6 — priced scope / budget / procurement signal
- E7 — paid work activated
- E8 — accepted delivery / payment
- E9 — repeat, expansion or referral caused by delivered value

Activity does not upgrade evidence. E4+ requires real human buyer evidence.

## Validation gate

The flagship advances only when the current threshold is met:

- 3 qualified conversations;
- 2 independent confirmations of recurring pain;
- 1 concrete commercial commitment.

If a segment repeatedly fails the underlying pain / budget hypotheses, reduce or stop activity there rather than adding features.

## Measurement

Track privately:

- trigger;
- segment;
- account;
- source;
- buyer role;
- route;
- proof object;
- delivered / bounced / replied;
- E-level;
- pain / objection;
- scope / budget signal;
- next action;
- eventual paid / repeat outcome.

Public repo reporting may contain only sanitized aggregate learning.

## Engine decision rule

Automate a step only when it is:
1. repeated,
2. deterministic enough to automate safely,
3. measurable,
4. cheaper or faster after automation,
5. not dependent on qualified regulatory judgment.

The goal is not the maximum number of tools. It is the highest commercial learning and accepted client value per unit of time and cost.
