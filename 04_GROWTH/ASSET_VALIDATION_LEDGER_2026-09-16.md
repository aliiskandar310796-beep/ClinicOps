## External demand context used for this build

Current official sources show active operational-change surfaces rather than a static compliance environment:

- European Commission: four EUDAMED modules became mandatory from 28 May 2026, including Actor registration, UDI/Devices, Notified Bodies & Certificates and Market Surveillance.
- European Commission: the UDI/Devices module requires manufacturers to submit UDI/device information for devices placed on the EU market, with updated implementation documentation available through the EUDAMED Information Centre.
- EMA: the CTIS sponsor handbook is the reference document for sponsors using CTIS and was last updated 17 July 2026.

These are **E1 market/context signals only**. They support testing evidence-change workflows; they do not validate ClinicOps demand or the new assets.

## Scale instrumentation to capture from first paid cases

For every activated Change Control Pack, privately record:

- number of declared surfaces;
- number and type of source/evidence inputs;
- preparation minutes;
- deterministic reconciliation minutes;
- qualified-review minutes;
- unresolved rows at first pass;
- exception categories;
- client clarification cycles;
- rework minutes;
- elapsed turnaround;
- accepted/returned/rejected deliverable state;
- whether cadence/repeat need exists;
- whether the common JSON/CSV/HTML shape held without bespoke reconstruction.

Scalability is not a feeling. It is established only when these measurements show stable structure and improving marginal delivery economics without weakening review quality.
