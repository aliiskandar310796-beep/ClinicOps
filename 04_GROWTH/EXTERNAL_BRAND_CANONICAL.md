## Company-page About

ClinicOps is an EU MedTech regulatory data integrity practice.

When an approved product or regulatory fact changes, the same fact may need to remain aligned across EUDAMED, UDI records, certificates, SS(C)P, IFUs, labels, declarations, RIM/master data and controlled spreadsheets.

ClinicOps compares the evidence deterministically and returns a source-linked exception queue: what aligns, what differs, what evidence is missing, who owns the next action and what closure evidence is needed.

Machines compare. Qualified humans interpret and decide.

ClinicOps does not replace the manufacturer's RA/QA team, RIM, QMS, PLM, ERP, a notified body or a competent authority. Missing public information is not treated as proof of non-compliance.

Primary engagement: Regulatory Change Integrity Review.
Entry engagement: Portfolio Integrity Scan.
Recurring monitoring is offered only where repeated portfolio drift makes it useful.

Denmark market access, Danish localisation and Danish pharmacovigilance remain bounded specialist capabilities.

Founder-led by Ali Iskandar, MSc in Medicine with Industrial Specialisation, with prior pharmacovigilance, CRO and regulated medical-documentation experience.

## Founder About — core positioning paragraph

I run ClinicOps, an EU MedTech regulatory data integrity practice. The work starts where approved facts are copied into several regulated surfaces and then change on different release cycles: EUDAMED, UDI data, certificates, SS(C)P, IFUs, labels and controlled records.

ClinicOps does not make automated compliance determinations. We structure the evidence, compare it deterministically, surface mismatches or missing evidence, and return a reviewer-ready closure queue so the responsible RA/QA team can decide and close the work.

My direct specialist depth includes Danish medical/regulatory documentation and pharmacovigilance operations. I use that as a bounded capability inside a broader EU MedTech data-integrity workflow, not as a claim to replace client regulatory ownership.

- approved / rejected;
- severity score / severity rating;
