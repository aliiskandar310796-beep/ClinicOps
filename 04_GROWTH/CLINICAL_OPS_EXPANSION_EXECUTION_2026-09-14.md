# ClinicOps clinical-operations expansion execution — 2026-09-14

Status: ACTIVE EXPERIMENT PORTFOLIO — adjacent lanes are not yet commercially validated.

## Strategic identity

ClinicOps is an evidence-controlled operating layer for clinical work.

The current commercial beachhead remains EU medical-device regulatory/change integrity. Expansion is allowed only when the workflow is credibly part of at least one of these domains:

- clinical research / study operations;
- medical-product quality and lifecycle operations;
- clinical technology / AI operations;
- diagnostic or medical-laboratory operations;
- clinic / care-delivery operations;
- controlled medical-content operations.

A generic GRC, finance, cyber, HR or enterprise workflow fails the ClinicOps name-fit gate unless it directly controls a clinical workflow or clinical evidence chain.

## Fleet topology

The fleet is a set of bounded execution lanes under the existing ClinicOps agent system, not a second permanent agent hierarchy.

### Fleet A — TrialOps

Mission: prove whether protocol/study-change propagation and evidence reconciliation create repeated buyer pain.

Manual paid entry offer:

**Protocol & Study-Change Integrity Review**

Minimum viable scope:
- one approved amendment/change event;
- one sponsor/CRO/study team;
- 3–10 declared affected surfaces;
- one named qualified decision owner;
- evidence/discrepancy register + reviewer queue + closure packet.

Candidate recurring stream:
- amendment/change register maintenance;
- periodic study/site evidence reconciliation;
- change-watch service tied to declared study surfaces.

Candidate low-touch stream after proof:
- browser-local amendment propagation mapper;
- study-change evidence packet template/license;
- standardized reviewer packet generation.

Validation gate:
- 3 qualified buyer conversations;
- 2 independent confirmations of repeated change-propagation/reconciliation pain;
- 1 E6 commercial signal or stronger;
- no critical unresolved delivery/control risk.

### Fleet B — QualityOps / QMS

**QMS Change-Propagation Review**

Minimum viable scope:
- one approved SOP/CAPA/change event;
- 3–10 affected controlled surfaces;
- training/supplier/system evidence where placed in scope;

Candidate recurring stream:
- controlled-change monitoring;

- periodic QMS evidence-integrity review.

Candidate low-touch stream after proof:

- evidence packet generator;
- controlled-change register subscription.

External E1 signals supporting the experiment:
- FDA states QMSR became effective 2026-02-02 and incorporates ISO 13485:2016 by reference.
- FDA states it began using the updated medical-device manufacturer inspection program on the same date and stopped using QSIT.
- FDA QMSR FAQ says investigators may review QMS records created before 2026-02-02 and notes that a comparative analysis may be useful for demonstrating prior records meet QMSR requirements.

Primary public evidence:
- https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/postmarket-requirements-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr
- https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/quality-management-system-regulation-qmsr/quality-management-system-regulation-frequently-asked-questions

These are market/operational signals only. They do not prove demand for ClinicOps.

Validation gate: same commercial threshold as Fleet A.

## Comparative scorecard

Every lane is scored monthly using actual evidence, not enthusiasm.

| Dimension | Weight | Evidence required |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| name / brand fit | 10 | direct connection to clinical work |
| repeated operational pain | 20 | E4/E5 buyer evidence |
| budget / activation signal | 20 | E6+ evidence |
| reuse of existing ClinicOps primitives | 15 | real workflow overlap |
| recurring revenue potential | 15 | repeated cadence confirmed |
| low-touch product potential | 10 | stable repeated structure after manual delivery |
| delivery risk / expertise gap | -10 | subtract for unresolved risk |

No lane becomes a default offer because of a high internal score alone. Commercial evidence controls promotion.

## Revenue architecture

The revenue ladder is deliberately sequenced:

1. **Active diagnostic/review** — bounded paid evidence review.
2. **Remediation / implementation support** — only after the decision owner confirms the accepted state.
3. **Recurring control** — periodic monitoring/reconciliation on a declared population.
4. **Partner delivery** — white-label or referral delivery where the partner retains the primary client relationship.
5. **Low-touch product** — templates, local tools, licenses and packet generators only after repeatability is proven.

Do not call downloads, page views, synthetic examples or CI runs passive income. Revenue requires actual commercial activation and payment evidence.

## Product spine

Every ClinicOps product should reuse these primitives where possible:

`approved source -> scope -> observation -> evidence reference -> deterministic state -> unresolved queue -> named decision owner -> human decision -> closure evidence -> portable audit trail`

Stable primitives can be automated. Authority remains human.

## Execution doctrine

`DISCOVER -> TEST -> SELL MANUALLY -> PROVE REPEATABILITY -> AUTOMATE -> MONITOR -> RETIRE OR EXPAND`

Kill or reshape a lane when:
- ten qualified conversations yield no E6 signal;
- buyers do not describe repeated reconciliation/change pain;
- delivery depends on expertise ClinicOps cannot responsibly provide;
- the work cannot be bounded around evidence/control rather than reserved clinical/regulatory/legal judgement;
- acquisition cost or founder effort makes the economics structurally unattractive.

Expand a lane when:
- E6/E7 evidence appears;
- the same evidence objects recur across two or more independent customers;
- a recurring cadence exists;
- automation reduces reconciliation effort without shifting decision authority to the machine.
