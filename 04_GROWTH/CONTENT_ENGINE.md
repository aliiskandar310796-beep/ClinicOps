# ClinicOps Content Engine

## Purpose
Convert regulatory intelligence into trust, visibility and qualified opportunities.

## Pipeline

Research signal
→ validated insight
→ research note
→ website article
→ LinkedIn post
→ newsletter
→ lead magnet
→ sales conversation

## Rules

- Evidence before publication.
- Separate observation, inference and recommendation.
- Never publish unsupported regulatory conclusions.
- Every content asset should map to a customer problem.

## Core themes

- EU MDR transition
- EUDAMED operations
- SS(C)P lifecycle management
- Basic UDI-DI intelligence
- Authorised Representative operations
- Regulatory intelligence workflows

## Measurement

Track:

- impressions
- qualified visitors
- inbound questions
- assessment requests
- conversion rate
- customer learning
