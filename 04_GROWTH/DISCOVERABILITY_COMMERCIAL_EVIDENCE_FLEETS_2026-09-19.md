# FLEET A — SEARCH / DISCOVERABILITY

Mission ID: **DISCOVERY-8**

Accountable owner: **Visibility Architect**

Control partners:

- Regulatory Evidence Steward — claim/source validity
- Release Sentinel — technical/indexing verification
- Opportunity Architect — buyer/query intent and commercial relevance

Temporary worker cap: **3 active by default, 5 absolute**

## 2. Objective

Move ClinicOps from weak/stale external discovery to an **8/10 discoverability state** in which current positioning is consistently surfaced, priority pages are indexable/indexed, non-branded high-intent query families produce visibility, and at least one qualified buyer action can be connected to search/discovery evidence.

Do not optimize for page count, social impressions or generic traffic.

---

## 3. Current observed external problem

As of 2026-09-19, external search evidence shows:

- ClinicOps LinkedIn content is being crawled and indexed rapidly.
- Search results can surface **multiple ClinicOps company identities** with inconsistent positioning, company size/location and stale claims.
- The production site itself is correctly deployed on the current GitHub Pages architecture, but external discovery is not reliably reflecting the latest site identity.
- Current discoverability therefore has an **entity-consistency + indexing + non-branded query** problem before it has a content-volume problem.

Treat this as P0 acquisition debt.

---

## 4. Discoverability score — 10 gates

The score is the number of verified gates below. A gate requires evidence; internal completion does not count.

### D1 — Production identity
Current homepage / Solution / About / Scanner are live on the expected production URLs and match the current positioning.

### D2 — Canonical entity consistency
Website, major LinkedIn company surface(s), founder profile and other controlled public profiles use one consistent current identity, description, URL and current claims.

Duplicate/stale ClinicOps company identities are reconciled, retired, redirected or clearly de-emphasized where the platform permits.

### D3 — Priority indexing
At least 90% of the priority URL set is confirmed indexed or actively discoverable by a major search engine.

Priority set:
- /
- /services.html
- /integrity-scanner.html
- /about.html
- /use-cases.html
- /eudamed-transition.html
- /sscp-operations.html
- /medical-device-document-control.html
- /denmark-market-access.html
- /research.html

### D4 — Branded SERP correctness
A branded ClinicOps search primarily exposes the current MedTech regulatory-data-integrity identity rather than obsolete clinic-compliance or contradictory profile language.

### D5 — Non-branded query visibility
ClinicOps earns impressions/visibility for at least **5 high-intent non-branded query families** relevant to the flagship, such as:
- EUDAMED data integrity / reconciliation
- UDI data integrity / reconciliation
- SS(C)P operations / version alignment
- medical device document control / change reconciliation
- regulatory change impact / evidence reconciliation
- Danish medical-device labeling / IFU integrity

Exact query families must be derived from observed buyer/search language, not keyword-volume theatre.

### D6 — Multi-page organic discovery
At least **3 distinct commercial/research landing pages** receive non-branded search impressions or equivalent independently observed search visibility.

### D7 — Search-to-proof path
Priority search landing pages route clearly into one useful proof object:
- Scanner
- specimen
- research
- review format
- intake

No informational dead ends.

### D8 — External authority signal
At least one relevant external surface beyond ClinicOps-owned social profiles links to, cites, or meaningfully references a ClinicOps proof/research asset.

A low-quality directory link does not satisfy this gate.

### D9 — Freshness / change propagation
When positioning or material public claims change, website metadata, structured data, sitemap, controlled social/company profiles and major discovery surfaces have an explicit reconciliation procedure.

### D10 — Qualified discovery action
At least one qualified buyer action has evidence reasonably attributable to search/discovery:
- inbound scope request mentioning search;
- buyer conversation referencing discovered research/tool;
- referral from an indexed external citation;
- another documented qualified discovery event.

Traffic alone does not satisfy D10.

## 6. Discoverability fleet workers

### Worker A1 — Entity Reconciliation Operator
Specialty: `distribution-operator`

Objective:
reconcile ClinicOps-controlled external identity surfaces against the current canonical positioning.

Checks:
- company name
- tagline
- company size
- location
- website URL
- founder attribution
- flagship description
- stale SLA language
- obsolete product names
- stale broad consultancy positioning

Success:
one consistent current entity representation across controllable high-impact surfaces.

Stop:
when all accessible controlled surfaces are reconciled or the remaining action is a reserved/manual platform action with an exact decision packet.

### Worker A2 — Search Intent + Landing Map
Specialty: `market-scout`

Objective:
map observed high-intent non-branded query families to the smallest number of existing ClinicOps pages that can answer them well.

Rules:
- improve existing high-value pages before creating new pages;
- no thin SEO page factories;
- pair the unfamiliar category "regulatory data integrity" with buyer language;
- preserve claim boundaries;
- research intent from actual search results, buyer language and source ecosystems.

Success:
each priority query family has one canonical landing page, one answer-first section and one proof/conversion path.

Stop:
when all chosen query families have a canonical page or evidence says the query is not worth targeting.

### Worker A3 — Indexing + Authority Operator
Specialty: `distribution-operator`

Objective:
move priority pages from technically live to externally discoverable.

Actions where tools/permissions allow:
- validate robots, sitemap, canonicals and metadata;
- inspect live page identity;
- prepare/execute search-console indexing steps within authority;
- identify stale/obsolete results;
- use validated LinkedIn research distribution as a bridge to canonical website assets;
- identify legitimate citation/link opportunities from partners, communities, research references and professional resources;
- verify external result after action.

Success:
D3, D4, D6 and D8 evidence improves.

Stop:
when remaining work requires a reserved account action or repeated submissions add no information.

### Rotating control worker — Discoverability Metrics Auditor
Specialty: `metrics-auditor`

Activate at review points, not continuously.

Objective:
score D1–D10 from evidence and reject vanity substitutions.

## 7. Discoverability execution loops

### Loop A — Entity cleanup
controlled surface -> compare to canonical site -> fix stale identity -> verify indexed/external representation.

### Loop B — Query capture
observed query/buyer language -> canonical answer page -> internal links -> proof asset -> CTA -> external search check.

### Loop C — Research authority
primary-source research -> bounded ClinicOps finding -> useful page -> LinkedIn/partner distribution -> external citation -> search visibility -> buyer action.

### Loop D — Stale index correction
obsolete result -> identify source -> canonical/metadata/update/removal request -> verify change.

---

## 8. Discoverability kill rules

Kill or redesign an SEO/content lane when:

- page is indexed but receives no relevant impressions after a meaningful observation period;
- traffic is non-buyer / irrelevant;
- repeated posts create impressions but no search or buyer movement;
- a query family maps poorly to the flagship;
- content requires weaker claims than ClinicOps can responsibly make;
- the page duplicates another canonical intent.

Do not create content merely to keep the fleet busy.

## 10. Commercial evidence score — 10 gates

### C1 — Explicit ICP
One current primary ICP is documented tightly enough to build an account list:
EU-market MedTech manufacturers with meaningful regulatory-record/document reconciliation burden and a reachable RA/QA/UDI owner.

### C2 — Qualified reachable accounts
At least 10 qualified reachable accounts/routes exist with evidence of fit.

This is E2, not demand.

### C3 — Verified demand tests
At least 6 high-quality demand tests are delivered to qualified routes without duplication/prohibited outreach.

This is E3, not demand.

### C4 — Buyer response
At least 3 qualified human buyer responses/conversations exist with private evidence references.

This is E4.

### C5 — Repeated pain
At least 2 independent buyers confirm substantially the same repeated workflow/pain/consequence in their own terms.

This is E5.

### C6 — Workflow economics / consequence
For at least 2 qualified buyers, evidence records:
- current workaround
- responsible owner
- operational consequence
- trigger/urgency
- approval/budget path or why it is unknown

No invented ROI.

### C7 — Concrete commitment
At least one buyer reaches E6:
- priced-scope request;
- proposed pilot;
- procurement step;
- identifiable approval/budget owner;
- equivalent concrete commitment.

### C8 — Activated paid work
At least one standard engagement reaches **E7** through the commercial activation gate.

### C9 — Accepted delivery / payment
At least one opportunity reaches E8.

### C10 — Repeat / expansion / qualified referral
At least one opportunity reaches E9.

## 14. Initial commercial experiment

Primary buyer hypothesis:

> Mid-market EU MedTech RA/QA teams experience repeated manual reconciliation when one approved product/regulatory fact must remain consistent across EUDAMED/UDI data, certificates, SS(C)P and controlled documents after change.

Primary question pattern:

> When one approved regulated value changes, how does your team verify that the master/RIM record, EU-facing data and controlled documents all moved together?

One proof link maximum per first touch.

Default proof:
the most relevant Scanner/specimen/research asset for the hypothesized workflow.

Do not lead with AI.

Do not lead with fear.

Do not lead with "compliance automation."

## 16. Commercial kill / pivot conditions

Preserve the existing EXP-001 rule:

- 3 qualified conversations;
- 2 independent repeated-pain confirmations;
- 1 concrete commercial commitment.

- no priced-scope request;
- no repeated budgeted urgency;

- buyer
- problem
- offer
- packaging
- distribution

before building more product.
