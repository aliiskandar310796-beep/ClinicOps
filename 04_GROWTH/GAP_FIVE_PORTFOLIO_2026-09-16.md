## DMAIC and stop rules
DEFINE one buyer problem and smallest accepted deliverable per account.
MEASURE E2-E9 with private provenance; baseline unknown stays unknown. Track qualified conversations, repeated pain, scope requests, activation, onboarding time, reviewer effort, rework, contribution margin and repeat demand.
ANALYZE alternative adequacy, routing failure, missing budget, onboarding friction and delivery constraints separately.
IMPROVE the smallest reversible next action; do not test all variables at once.
CONTROL central ownership, mailbox+master suppression, atomic send reservations, claims, private evidence, approval and follow-up checks.

Review after five substantive target-buyer conversations per lane. Seek two independent repeated-pain confirmations plus a concrete priced-scope/budget/procurement step; this is a signal, not product validation. After ten qualified conversations without willingness to sponsor or repeated budgeted pain, pause/change buyer, offer or channel. Silence, opens and automated replies neither validate nor falsify the market. Existing product-extraction and scalability gates remain unchanged.

A customer is E7 only: written paid scope/acceptance and approved payment/deposit/PO activation with private evidence. Do not count the same organisation five times across lanes. E8/E9 are acceptance/payment and repeat/expansion evidence, respectively.
