An SS(C)P and a EUDAMED device record describe the same device. The join key between them is the Basic UDI-DI.

Article 32(2)(a) MDR requires the summary of safety and clinical performance to identify the device, including its Basic UDI-DI. That makes the identifier the reconciliation key between the document a manufacturer publishes and the record EUDAMED holds.

In a September 2026 public-source convenience sample, the Basic UDI-DI printed in the published SS(C)P matched no Basic UDI-DI in EUDAMED in 6 of 22 consecutive rows. Small, non-random sample; aggregates only; no manufacturer named; "match" means identifiers, never document text. Not a compliance finding — a reconciliation signal for a qualified reviewer.

Why now: MDCG 2026-4 describes a Production deployment planned for October 2026 for the revised SS(C)P operating model, and recommends uploading SS(C)Ps for devices already on the market no later than 27 February 2027 — a recommendation in a non-binding paper, not a statutory deadline.

One check before then: does the Basic UDI-DI on page one of every SS(C)P equal the one on the EUDAMED record?
