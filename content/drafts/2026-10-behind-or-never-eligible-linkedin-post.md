When a published SS(C)P and its EUDAMED record disagree, there are two stories. The public record cannot tell them apart.

Story one: the record is behind. A newer revision exists; the register shows the earlier one.

Story two: the published revision was never eligible to appear. Under the document-control model MDCG 2019-9 rev.1 describes, an updated revision can exist while notified-body validation timing is in process, so a revision may legitimately be published between validation cycles. Some manufacturers print this themselves: a revision history marking the current revision as not validated.

In the September 2026 public-source scan, in 22 consecutive rows no published SS(C)P and EUDAMED record carried the same reference number and revision. That count says nothing about which story each row is; it is not an irregularity count.

The structure: one outcome column with at least three values ("record behind", "published between cycles", "unresolved"), decided from the manufacturer's own revision history, never from the public side.

Manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers and notified bodies then argue about facts, not labels.

Convenience sample; aggregates only; nobody named.
