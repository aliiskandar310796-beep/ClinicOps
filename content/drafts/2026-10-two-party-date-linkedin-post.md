27 February 2027 is often filed as a manufacturer task. MDCG 2026-4 frames it as a date two parties plan together.

For devices placed on the market before mandatory use, the paper says manufacturers "should upload SS(C)Ps in EUDAMED as soon as possible, and no later than 27 February 2027", "considering that manufacturers and notified bodies should align on timelines to fulfil their registration obligations".

The paper's views "are not legally binding". This is a recommendation, not a statutory deadline.

The failure mode is two plans built separately: a manufacturer schedule with an upload date, a notified-body schedule with its own steps, and no row where the two are compared. Each plan looks complete on its own.

One shared row per device closes the gap: Basic UDI-DI, SS(C)P reference and revision, the notified body's step and date, the manufacturer's step and date, and the source of every date.

Authorised representatives and importers following the same portfolio can then read one plan instead of reconciling two.
