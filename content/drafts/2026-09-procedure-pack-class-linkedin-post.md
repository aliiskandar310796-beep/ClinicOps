A class III value on a EUDAMED record does not always mean a class III device.

System and procedure pack registrations (PR-role records) carry the highest risk class among the devices that form part of the pack. The value is inherited from a constituent device; it is not an ordinary classification of the pack itself.

That matters for anyone screening a portfolio for SS(C)P coverage. Under Article 22 MDR, a person who places a pack on the market under Article 22(1) or (3) is not thereby the manufacturer of the constituent devices, and Article 32 places the SS(C)P duty on the manufacturer. On ClinicOps' reading — a derivation, not a stated Commission or MDCG position — no SS(C)P duty arises for such a pack, while the constituent devices keep their own manufacturers' obligations.

The failure mode: filter a register by risk class, see a class III record without a linked SS(C)P, log a gap. Role first, class second.

Screening that keeps manufacturer, authorised-representative, importer and pack-producer roles explicit does not invent that gap.
