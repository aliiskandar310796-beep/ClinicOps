What does an MDR distributor actually have to verify? A recent ruling draws the line more precisely than many SOPs assume.

In Case C-10/24, Dürr Dental (CJEU, 4 June 2026), the Court of Justice clarified the scope of the distributor's due-care duty under Article 14 MDR.

The headline for distributors and importers: you are not required to independently determine a device's risk class. Your duty is a consistency check against the information already available to you — not a second conformity assessment, and not a re-run of the manufacturer's classification.

Three points worth carrying into your procedures:

- A distributor does not have to independently re-classify the device. The manufacturer remains the duty holder for classification and conformity.
- The four-digit notified-body number is checked only where the manufacturer's own classification implies that a notified body is involved. It is a consistency check, not a standalone verification duty triggered "whenever one is required."
- The instructions for use sit here as one input to that consistency check — a source of information — not the object of the operative ruling.

If your distributor and importer SOPs currently instruct staff to "verify the risk class" or "confirm the notified-body number in every case," this ruling is a reason to re-read that wording.
