Ahead of 28 November 2026, many portfolio trackers carry one column: "EUDAMED done". It hides three separate questions.

1. Registration. The Commission's transition timeline gives 28 November 2026 as the deadline to register, in the UDI/Devices module, devices already on the market before mandatory use, following Decision (EU) 2025/2371. The Decision itself does not print the date.

2. Scope. Under MDR Article 32(1), the manufacturer draws up an SS(C)P for implantable and class III devices, subject to the Article 32 exclusions. A registered device may or may not be in scope.

3. Identity. Article 32(2)(a) requires the SS(C)P to identify the device, including the Basic UDI-DI. Where an MDR SS(C)P exists, the identifier it prints is a value to check against the registration.

The failure mode: a row marked done when only question 1 was answered, or a row flagged because question 2 was never asked.

Keep three columns, each with a source and a named owner, so manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers and notified bodies reading the same tracker can see which question is still open.
