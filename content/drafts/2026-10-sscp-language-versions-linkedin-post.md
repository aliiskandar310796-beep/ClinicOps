Under MDR Article 32(1), the manufacturer draws up an SS(C)P for implantable and class III devices, subject to the Article 32 exclusions. Across EU markets, that one obligation often reaches readers as several language files.

So before reconciling anything, answer a document-control question: is each language version a translation of one controlled document, or a controlled document of its own, with its own reference number and revision counter?

The answer changes what a comparison means. If the versions share one revision scale, "Rev. 3" in one language next to "Rev. 5" in another is worth a reviewer's look. If they do not, the same pair says nothing about drift.

In a September 2026 public-source convenience sample (69 device rows, ~42 manufacturers publishing national-language SS(C)Ps), 5 of 7 multi-language document sets were identical across languages. Small sample, aggregates only, metadata compared, not text.

Map every language file to the SS(C)P its identifiers say it is, then compare.
