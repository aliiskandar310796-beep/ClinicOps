Three EUDAMED dates sit on many transition plans. They are not the same kind of date.

28 November 2026: the Commission's transition timeline gives this as the deadline to register, in the UDI/Devices module, devices already on the market before mandatory use, following Decision (EU) 2025/2371. The Decision itself does not print the date.

27 February 2027: MDCG 2026-4 says manufacturers "should upload SS(C)Ps in EUDAMED as soon as possible, and no later than 27 February 2027" for devices placed on the market before mandatory use. A recommendation in a paper whose views "are not legally binding".

October 2026: MDCG 2026-4 describes a Production deployment planned for October 2026 for the revised SS(C)P model. A plan, not an obligation. Check the Commission's current status before scheduling work against it.

The failure mode: one "deadline" column holding a Commission-stated consequence, a non-binding recommendation and a release plan. Manufacturers, authorised representatives, importers and notified bodies then plan against different readings.

Give each date its source and its type.
