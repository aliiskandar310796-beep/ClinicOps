# Who does what in the revised EUDAMED SS(C)P model: a role-by-role reconciliation note on MDCG 2026-4

## Why this note

MDCG 2026-4 describes a Production deployment planned for October 2026 for a revised EUDAMED SS(C)P operating model [1]. A plan in a non-binding position paper can move, so check the Commission's current EUDAMED status before relying on any date in this note. What does not depend on the date is the allocation of tasks, and that allocation decides which records have to agree.

## The allocation (primary, non-binding)

MDCG 2026-4 describes a model in which the manufacturer uploads the SS(C)P master version and translations, while the notified body indicates the validated SS(C)P against the relevant Basic UDI-DI(s) [2]. In the paper's words, the revised functionality "will require the notified body to indicate which is the validated SSCP(s), or parts thereof, in accordance with Article 32(1) MDR, by ticking the box(es) corresponding to the relevant Basic UDI-DI(s)", and "It is the responsibility of the manufacturer to ensure that the uploaded SSCP is the one validated by the notified body during the certification process." [2]

Two anchors sit underneath. Under Article 32(1) MDR the manufacturer draws up the SS(C)P for implantable and class III devices, subject to the Article 32 exclusions [3]. Article 32(2)(a) requires the SS(C)P to identify the device, including the Basic UDI-DI [4].

## What has to agree

The Basic UDI-DI therefore appears in three places that different actors control:

1. the identification section of the SS(C)P itself (the manufacturer's document, Article 32(2)(a));
2. the uploaded file in EUDAMED (the manufacturer's upload);
3. the Basic UDI-DI box(es) the notified body ticks for the validated SS(C)P.

A reconciliation that keeps the roles explicit asks one question per role:

- **Manufacturer (MF):** for each SS(C)P, which Basic UDI-DIs does the document print, and is the file to be uploaded the version the notified body validated?
- **Notified body:** do the Basic UDI-DIs to be ticked equal the set printed in the validated document?
- **Authorised representative (AR), importer (IM), system/procedure-pack producer (PR):** the passages quoted above assign the upload to the manufacturer and the indication to the notified body. A portfolio review from these roles should record any open item against the actor the paper names, not against its own role.

## Timing

For devices placed on the market before mandatory use, MDCG 2026-4 says manufacturers "should upload SS(C)Ps in EUDAMED as soon as possible, and no later than 27 February 2027" [5]. This is a recommendation in a paper whose views "are not legally binding", not a statutory deadline.

## Boundaries

This note summarises a non-binding MDCG position paper and two Articles of the MDR. It makes no statement about any named party's registration, SS(C)P, conformity or CE marking. A difference between a document and a record is a reconciliation item for a qualified reviewer, not a compliance finding. Missing public metadata is never, by itself, a finding.
