28 November 2026 is the Commission's stated deadline to register, in the EUDAMED UDI/Devices module, devices already on the market before mandatory use — the Commission timeline figure following Decision (EU) 2025/2371, not a date printed in the Decision itself.

A fair question for a manufacturer, an authorised representative or an importer: "Are all our marketed devices actually in there?"

What the public EUDAMED record could not do for you in September 2026, on ClinicOps' observation: the riskClass and legislation query parameters were ignored; the same unfiltered query returned different totals minutes apart; trade-name search is an unanchored substring match; there is no Basic UDI-DI lookup. Point-in-time behaviour, not a service contract — and never a basis for claiming full-register coverage.

So coverage reconciliation runs the other way. Start from your authoritative device list, expect one record per device, compare, and treat every unresolved row as "missing evidence" rather than "missing registration". Whether a given device is a legacy device remains the manufacturer's determination.
