## Core distinction

The SRN is EU-wide actor identification following competent-authority validation. It does **not** by itself mean that the AR has accepted a written mandate for any particular manufacturer/device. MDR/IVDR Article 11 obligations must be addressed before accepting a mandate. The manufacturer retains its applicable responsibilities.

Sources: [EC Actor Registration](https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/actor-registration-module_en); [EUDAMED mandates](https://webgate.ec.europa.eu/eudamed-help/en/actor/manage/manage-mandates.html); MDR Regulation (EU) 2017/745 Article 11; IVDR Regulation (EU) 2017/746 Article 11.
