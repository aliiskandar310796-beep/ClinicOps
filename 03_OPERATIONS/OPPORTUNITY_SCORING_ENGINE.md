# ClinicOps Opportunity Scoring Engine

## Purpose

Prioritise opportunities by expected strategic value, not by excitement.

Every opportunity should be scored before significant build effort.

## Score model

Opportunity Score = Pain × Urgency × Evidence × Buyer Access × Revenue Potential

Each dimension: 1-5

## Dimensions

### Pain
1 = unclear problem
5 = urgent operational/regulatory pain

### Urgency
1 = no trigger
5 = deadline, regulation change, certificate transition, customer event

### Evidence
1 = assumption only
5 = repeated evidence from primary sources, customers, or data

### Buyer Access
1 = unknown buyer
5 = direct relationship or clear channel

### Revenue Potential
1 = low value
5 = repeatable high-value offer

## Decision rules

20-25: Prioritise

12-19: Run small experiment

<12: Archive or gather more evidence

## Required before execution

Every opportunity needs:

- target buyer
- problem statement
- evidence
- smallest test
- success metric
- kill condition
- learning captured

## Principle

The goal is not maximum ideas. The goal is maximum validated learning velocity.
