# ClinicOps Revenue OS

## Purpose

Turn regulatory intelligence into a repeatable commercial learning system.

Operating loop:

Signal → Opportunity → Hypothesis → Experiment → Evidence → Decision → Asset → Revenue

## Principles

- Do not build without a learning objective.
- Every opportunity needs a buyer, pain, evidence, test, metric and kill condition.
- Preserve failed experiments as institutional knowledge.
- Prefer reusable assets over one-off work.

## Opportunity Record

Each opportunity should contain:

- ID
- Date identified
- Source signal
- Buyer segment
- Problem/pain
- Why now
- Evidence level
- Proposed offer
- Smallest experiment
- Success metric
- Kill condition
- Result
- Learning
- Next action

## Buyer Segments

### Authorised Representatives

### Regulatory Consultants

- portfolio analytics

## Current Experiments

### REV-001: AR Transition Intelligence Pilot

Hypothesis:
ARs need portfolio-level transition prioritisation rather than more generic regulatory information.

Test:
Present anonymised transition methodology to selected ARs.

Measure:
- interest
- meetings
- objections
- willingness to pay

Kill condition:
No meaningful pain after repeated discovery conversations.

### REV-002: Research Visibility Flywheel

Hypothesis:
Evidence-backed research creates stronger inbound authority than generic marketing.

Test:
Convert validated research into articles, LinkedIn content and partner briefs.

Measure:
- qualified conversations
- engagement from target buyers

Automate:

Do not automate:

- sales claims without evidence
