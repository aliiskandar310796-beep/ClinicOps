# ClinicOps Expert Network Governance

Status: ACTIVE INTERNAL CONTROL

## Purpose
ClinicOps may assemble project teams from independent clinical researchers, life-science/MDR/clinical-trial consultants, Danish physicians, nurses and other specialists when a real engagement requires that competence. The network expands capability and resilience without implying that every expert is an employee, officer, standing representative or decision-maker for ClinicOps.

## Default model
- Maintain a private, consent-based roster rather than a public staff directory by default.
- Appoint experts per project only after scope, credentials, availability, conflicts, compensation, confidentiality and data access are agreed.
- Publish a named expert profile only with explicit consent and verified facts.
- Never market access to a title as if it transfers clinical, regulatory, legal, safety, quality or release authority to ClinicOps.
- Default to non-patient-facing consulting and evidence work. Direct healthcare delivery requires a separate governance review before any commitment.

## Minimum private registry
For each expert record only what is necessary:
- preferred name and contact route;
- professional role and specialty;
- country/jurisdiction;
- verified education/qualifications;
- professional authorisation/licence status where relevant, source and verification date;
- whether independent-practice status is required for the proposed task;
- relevant experience and permitted work types;
- Danish/English/other language capability;
- availability;
- conflict declaration date and known exclusions;
- confidentiality/data-processing terms;
- contracting/payment classification;
- professional indemnity or other insurance where relevant;
- public-profile consent and exact approved public wording;
- current status: AVAILABLE / LIMITED / PAUSED / DO_NOT_ASSIGN.

Do not store unnecessary sensitive information in the roster.

## Assignment gate
An expert may be assigned only when all are true:
1. The engagement has a written scope and accountable client/project owner.
2. The expert's competence is relevant to the specific task.
3. Credentials and any required authorisation are current and verified.
4. Conflict and independence checks are complete.
5. Confidentiality, IP, data-access and compensation terms are agreed.
6. The task states whether the expert advises, reviews or has any reserved decision role.
7. Required insurance or professional cover has been checked for the task.
8. Patient-level or special-category data access is minimized and separately governed.

## Danish healthcare-professional boundary
A Danish medical authorisation and permission to practise independently are distinct statuses. For physicians, permission for independent practice follows the applicable clinical-basis-training and authorisation process; do not infer it from the title "doctor" or KBU participation alone. Verify current status against Styrelsen for Patientsikkerhed / Autorisationsregisteret when the task requires it.

For nurses and other authorised professions, verify the relevant Danish authorisation status when the assignment depends on regulated professional competence.

A physician or nurse may support non-patient-facing evidence, research, workflow, content or advisory work within competence without automatically becoming the clinical decision owner for a ClinicOps engagement.

## Direct-care kill switch
Do not sell or start direct patient treatment, diagnosis, prescribing, treatment-place operation or other healthcare-provider activity under the standard ClinicOps consulting model. Escalate first for a documented review of:
- treatment-place registration/supervision obligations;
- required independent-practice or other professional status;
- responsible clinician requirements;
- patient-safety duties;
- professional indemnity/insurance;
- medical-record/journal obligations;
- GDPR/health-data processing roles and agreements;
- incident and complaint handling;
- employer/contractor and tax classification.

## Contractor / freelancer classification
A contract label alone does not determine Danish tax or employment status. The factual relationship controls whether a person is treated as an employee, fee recipient or independent business. Before repeated or economically dependent work, review the actual working arrangement and use appropriate payroll/tax/accounting advice.

## Public representation rules
Allowed when verified and consented:
- "Independent consultant in the ClinicOps expert network"
- specific verified profession, qualification, language or specialty
- specific project role after appointment

Do not publish without a formal basis:
- employee, partner, officer, medical director, responsible physician or authorised representative;
- "ClinicOps doctor" or equivalent wording that implies a standing clinical role;
- regulatory, legal or compliance authority not actually held;
- client names or project outcomes without permission.

## Commercial use
The network is commercially useful when it shortens the path from a qualified workflow to the right expertise. It should support:
- project-specific clinical context;
- Danish-market language/context review;
- trial/site, MDR/quality and research expertise;
- specialist reviewer availability;
- resilience when one individual is unavailable;
- partner/co-delivery opportunities.

It should not be used as vanity headcount. Do not publish a large roster merely to look larger.

## Evidence and source references
Primary current sources for relevant Danish checks include:
- Styrelsen for Patientsikkerhed professional authorisation and Autorisationsregisteret;
- Styrelsen for Patientsikkerhed rules for independent medical practice and treatment-place governance;
- Skattestyrelsen guidance on employee / fee-recipient / independent-business classification;
- Datatilsynet for personal-data and health-data processing requirements.

Re-check current official sources before relying on a rule in a live engagement.
