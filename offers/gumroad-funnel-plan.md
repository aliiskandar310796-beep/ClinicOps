This copy inherits the ClinicOps claim rules and homepage disclaimers. In particular:

- The free **EUDAMED Identifier Check** screens **identifier structure only** — including B-prefixed legacy-style EUDAMED DI signals and common SRN actor-role shapes. - **No compliance allegations.** Checklists, templates and matrices are operational organising tools. A missing public field is never presented as a standalone non-compliance finding.
- Deliverables describe the **supplied/reachable portfolio evidence**, never "the entire public register."
- Priority/triage ordering is an **operator triage mechanism**, never a regulatory-risk or legal-conclusion score.
- Regulatory statements that assert a fact are tagged **[VERIFY: ...]** and map to the repository claim registry (claim IDs shown for internal traceability; **do not render claim IDs on the public Gumroad listings**).

## 1.1 EUDAMED Identifier Check (free browser tool)

**Product title:** EUDAMED Identifier Check — Free Structural Screening Tool

**Subtitle (one line):** Screen the *structure* of an EUDAMED identifier in your browser — including B-prefix legacy-style DI and common SRN actor-role shapes.

**Description (100–150 words):**
A free, local browser tool for anyone working with EU medical device identifiers. Paste an identifier and the Check screens its **structure** — flagging B-prefixed legacy-style EUDAMED DI signals, common SRN actor-role shapes, and check-digit structure — so you can quickly read what *kind* of identifier you are looking at. It runs client-side; nothing about your portfolio is uploaded. **What it does not do:** it does not verify current actor status, device responsibility, registration state, or compliance. A B-prefix is a useful structural screening signal, but it does not by itself establish a device's full regulatory or SS(C)P state. Use it as a fast first read, then decide what the identifier implies for your transition work plan — the paid layer answers that question with evidence and accountable human review.

**What you get:**
- Instant, browser-based structural screening of a single identifier
- B-prefix legacy-style EUDAMED DI signal detection
- Common SRN actor-role shape recognition (MF / AR / IM / PR shapes)
- Check-digit structure validation
- Runs locally in your browser — no portfolio data uploaded
- Plain-language explanation of what the structure does *and does not* tell you

**Next-step CTA:** *A structure signal raises a question it can't answer: what does this identifier mean for your portfolio's transition work?* → **Request a Transition Intelligence Assessment.**

## 1.2 MDR Transition Checklist

**Product title:** MDR Transition Checklist — Legacy-to-MDR Portfolio Worklist

**Subtitle (one line):** A structured checklist to organise legacy-to-MDR transition questions across a class III / implantable portfolio.

**Description (100–150 words):**
A practical, no-cost checklist for teams organising a legacy-to-MDR transition across a device portfolio. It walks you through the questions that actually drive a work plan: which items are still represented through a legacy registration path, where certificate and transition timing creates pressure, which identifiers need reconciliation, and which rows are blocked by missing evidence rather than by a decision. It is an **organising tool, not a compliance determination** — it helps you separate what you know from what you still need to confirm, and keeps machine-detected signals separate from RA/QA judgement. Nothing here alleges non-compliance; a blank field is a work item, not a finding. Use it to build your own worklist, then bring the unresolved rows to a scoped assessment where evidence and human review turn the list into a prioritised plan.

**What you get:**
- A sectioned transition worklist (registration path, certificate/transition timing, identifiers, SS(C)P workflow, market-language, ownership)
- "Known vs. still-to-confirm" columns that keep evidence gaps visible
- Prompts that separate machine-detected signals from RA/QA judgement
- Actor-role reminders (MF / AR / IM / PR are not interchangeable)
- Guidance framing every blank as a work item, not a compliance finding
- A tidy handoff shape you can bring straight into a scoping conversation

**Next-step CTA:** *Turned up rows you can't close on your own?* → **Request a Transition Intelligence Assessment** to prioritise them with evidence and human review.

## 1.3 SS(C)P Readiness Checklist

**Product title:** SS(C)P Readiness Checklist — Evidence & Operations Worklist

**Subtitle (one line):** Organise SS(C)P evidence, versions, translations and handoffs into a traceable readiness worklist.

**Description (100–150 words):**
SS(C)P work is more than a document upload — it sits across manufacturer responsibility, Basic UDI-DI relationships, master-version and translation control, notified-body validation context and downstream publication operations. This free checklist helps you organise that evidence into a readiness worklist without losing the trail. It prompts you to inventory versions and translations, record where each input came from, flag unresolved source conflicts, and map who owns each handoff. Under MDR Article 32, the manufacturer is the duty holder for drawing up the SSCP for applicable implantable and class III devices, subject to the Article 32 exclusions **[VERIFY: MDR Article 32 duty-holder scope and exclusions — claim CO-CLM-0001]**. This checklist supports that work; it does not replace it, and no missing public field is treated as a standalone non-compliance finding.

**What you get:**
- An SS(C)P evidence-inventory worklist (master versions, translations, Basic UDI-DI relationships)
- Provenance fields so every input records its source and date
- An unresolved-source-conflict register
- A role-and-handoff map (who owns each step)
- Publication-readiness prompts framed as questions, not verdicts
- A duty-holder-context reminder that keeps Article 32 responsibility explicit

**Next-step CTA:** *Ready to turn the worklist into a prioritised, evidence-backed plan?* → **Request a Transition Intelligence Assessment.**

**Suggested tier label:** *Operator pack* (self-serve)

**Description:**
A worksheet-driven matrix for segmenting a portfolio by registration path and turning that into an operator triage order. ## 2.3 SS(C)P Evidence & Translation Worksheet

**Suggested tier label:** *Operator pack* (self-serve)

**Price:** `[price — user to set / A/B test]`

**Description:**
A structured worksheet for teams managing several SS(C)Ps and their translations. It captures a master-version and translation inventory, Basic UDI-DI relationships, evidence provenance, unresolved source conflicts, and a role/handoff matrix, so an SS(C)P family stays traceable as it moves toward publication readiness. It also includes a reference note on the revised EUDAMED operating model described in MDCG 2026-4 — under which the manufacturer uploads the SS(C)P master version and translations while the notified body indicates the validated SS(C)P against the relevant Basic UDI-DI(s) — flagged as a **non-binding MDCG operating model that should be verified against current production behaviour before operational reliance [VERIFY: MDCG 2026-4 operating model — claim CO-CLM-0009]**. A "MATCH" on metadata means identifiers/dates align; it never means document bodies were compared. No missing public field is treated as a non-compliance finding.

**What's inside:** version & translation inventory worksheet · Basic UDI-DI relationship map · evidence-provenance fields · unresolved-source-conflict register · role/handoff matrix · MDCG 2026-4 operating-model reference note (non-binding).
