## Operating rules

- Prefer reversible experiments before building large systems.
- Capture negative results; killed ideas are organisational memory.
- Do not confuse activity, compliments, page views, or generic interest with buying evidence.
- Customer conversations are evidence, not validation by themselves.
- External claims must pass the claim registry before publication.
- For offer validation, record the buyer's actual portfolio context, repeated manual work, urgency, budget signal, objections, requested deliverable, and next commitment without putting private prospect data in the public repository.
- Do not expand the product surface because an experiment sounds promising. Expand only when a repeated paid-work friction or a quantified demand signal justifies it.

## Initial priority experiments

### EXP-001 — AR transition intelligence pilot
**Hypothesis:** authorised representatives, regulatory consultancies, and class III / implantable manufacturers will pay for portfolio-level legacy-to-MDR evidence reconciliation and prioritised transition work planning when the alternative is repeated manual reconciliation across EUDAMED, identifiers, certificate timing, SS(C)P evidence, and market-language document controls.

**Buyer / user:**
- EU authorised representatives managing multiple manufacturer portfolios;
- regulatory consultancies that could use a white-label evidence/work-plan layer;
- manufacturers with a material class III / implantable transition workload.

**Evidence basis:** the Class III Transition Map offer specification, the sanitized public sample, the browser-local assessment intake, and the current claim-governed portfolio tooling already make a scoped pilot deliverable possible without building more SaaS surface.

**Smallest useful test:** conduct three qualified portfolio conversations using the existing Transition Map sample and assessment intake. A qualified conversation must reach the buyer's real portfolio/workflow problem rather than remain at generic EUDAMED education. Capture whether they will discuss a portfolio boundary, repeated reconciliation work, timing pressure, evidence gaps, desired output, and the next commercial step.

**Success metric:**
1. three qualified portfolio conversations completed;
2. at least two buyers independently describe repeated portfolio reconciliation / evidence-control work that the current offer addresses; and
3. at least one buyer asks for a priced scope, proposes a pilot, identifies budget/approval ownership, or otherwise makes a concrete commercial commitment beyond general interest.

A stronger validation signal is one paid pilot followed by a repeat, expansion, referral, or request to reuse the workflow on another portfolio.

**Kill / change condition:** if ten qualified target-buyer conversations produce no concrete willingness to sponsor a pilot, no priced-scope request, and no repeated evidence of budgeted urgency, pause product expansion and change at least one of buyer, problem, offer, packaging, or distribution before building more functionality.

**Result:** pending. Do not mark validated from conversations alone; record the actual commercial commitments when they occur.

**Learning captured:** pending. Record objections and non-buying reasons as carefully as positive signals.

**Next action:** use the existing live sample, Readiness Score, and assessment-intake path to test the offer. Do not build a portal, dashboard, account ingestion flow, or new paid software tier before this experiment produces buying evidence.

### EXP-002 — Research-led visibility
**Hypothesis:** anonymised, method-stated regulatory research creates higher-quality inbound than generic service marketing.

**Smallest useful test:** publish one evidence-gated research asset only after the relevant external-publication approval, then measure whether it creates qualified regulatory conversations or assessment requests rather than vanity engagement.

**Success metric:** at least one qualified buyer/partner conversation or assessment request attributable to the research asset, with the source recorded privately where appropriate.

**Kill condition:** several approved publications generate no meaningful qualified signal; stop increasing publication volume and change topic, distribution, or buyer targeting before producing more content.

**Result:** pending.
