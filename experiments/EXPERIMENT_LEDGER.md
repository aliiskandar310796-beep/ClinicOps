## Operating rules

- Prefer reversible experiments before building large systems.
- Capture negative results; killed ideas are organisational memory.
- Do not confuse activity with learning.
- External claims must pass the claim registry before publication.
- Customer conversations are evidence, not validation by themselves.

## Initial priority experiments

### EXP-001 — AR transition intelligence pilot
Hypothesis: authorised representatives and regulatory partners will value portfolio-level legacy-to-MDR transition prioritisation more than generic compliance messaging.

Test: conduct targeted conversations using the transition map methodology.

Metric: qualified conversations, recurring pain signals, willingness to sponsor a pilot.

Kill condition: repeated evidence that the problem is not urgent or not budgeted.

### EXP-002 — Research-led visibility
Hypothesis: anonymised, method-stated regulatory research creates higher-quality inbound than generic service marketing.

Test: publish one evidence-gated research asset and measure engagement quality.

Metric: relevant contacts, partner conversations, requests for assessment.

Kill condition: no meaningful signal after several validated publications.
