# Discovery hypotheses

Status: untested. No hypothesis has evidence yet. Each is written to be falsified. "Confirm" and "kill" thresholds refer to ledger rows at E4 or above (see `evidence_rules.md`). Thresholds are working defaults for a solo founder and may be revised before interviews start, not after.

Minimum sample before any verdict: 10 genuine interviews per segment. Below that, the status stays "untested" or "inconclusive".

| # | Hypothesis | Confirmed if | Killed if |
|---|---|---|---|
| H1 | Manufacturer RA/QA teams reconcile approved changes across records by hand, at least quarterly. | 6+ of 10 describe a specific event in the last 12 months with named records and hours spent. | 6+ of 10 cannot recall a specific event, or say it is automated within their RIM/QMS. |
| H2 | The reconciliation takes material time (more than 2 person-days per event) and displaces other work. | 5+ of 10 give a concrete duration and headcount above that. | Most report under half a day per event. |
| H3 | Downstream mismatches are found late (after release, audit or a notified body query), not at the time of change. | 4+ of 10 describe a mismatch found after the fact, with a consequence. | Most say mismatches are caught at change closure by existing controls. |
| H4 | Current workarounds (spreadsheets, consultants) are seen as inadequate, and something has been tried. | 5+ of 10 name a workaround and a specific failure, or a prior attempt to improve. | Most are satisfied with the workaround and have never tried to change it. |
| H5 | A named owner exists and a budget path is identifiable for external help on this work. | 3+ of 10 name an owner and an approval route; 1+ has spent on it in 12 months. | No one can name an owner or budget line, or all say it is absorbed and unfunded. |
| H6 | Authorised representatives and consultancies with several manufacturers feel the pain more often than single manufacturers. | AR/consultancy interviews report events more often and at greater volume than manufacturers. | No difference, or manufacturers report more. |
| H7 | Buyers will accept an external, human-reviewed service on supplied exports without integrating with their systems. | 4+ of 10 say supplying an export for a bounded review is acceptable to their data and confidentiality rules. | Most say policy or confidentiality rules bar external review of supplied records. |
| H8 | The trigger for action is a specific event (change, audit, transition deadline), not general interest. | 5+ of 10 identify an event that made or would make them act within a defined period. | Interest is general and no event-linked timing appears. |

## Reading results
- A hypothesis confirmed in one segment says nothing about other segments.
- Do not count politeness, compliments or "interesting" as confirming evidence.
- If H1, H2 and H5 are killed for a segment, stop outreach to that segment.
- Record verdicts here with the ledger ids relied on, once the ledger has rows. Until then everything above is untested.
