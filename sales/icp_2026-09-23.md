# ICP - Regulatory Change Integrity Review (2026-09-23)

Internal sales working document. Not for public release. Commercial framing follows CLAIM_RULES.md: the opportunity is transition backlog and document-operation complexity, never manufacturer negligence or non-compliance. Nothing here is a finding about any named company.

## Offer (from docs/services.html)
Regulatory Change Integrity Review: one approved change (or a bounded portfolio) reconciled against EUDAMED, UDI data, certificates, SS(C)P, declarations, labels, IFUs and controlled records; output is a source-linked exception queue (Source / Expected / Observed / Status / Evidence / Owner / Next Action / Closure). No platform migration.

## Segment
EU-domiciled legal manufacturers of medical devices (MDR; IVDR secondary) in Germany, Netherlands, Ireland, Sweden, Belgium, France, Italy, Spain, Poland.
- Size: roughly 50-2,000 employees, with an in-house RA/QA team (about 3-40 people) but no dedicated regulatory-data operations function. Enterprises (e.g. Philips, B. Braun) are listed as low-probability, long-cycle accounts.
- Portfolio: dozens to thousands of UDI-DIs / SKUs, multiple language versions of labels/IFUs, several notified-body certificates.
- Best fit: Class III, implantable and Class IIb legacy-transition devices; software/IIa portfolios with frequent changes are secondary.

## Triggers (verified by search 2026-09-23)
1. EUDAMED: Commission Decision (EU) 2025/2371 of 26 Nov 2025, published OJ 27 Nov 2025; four modules (actor registration, UDI/device registration, notified bodies and certificates, market surveillance) mandatory from 28 May 2026 (already in force). Source: https://health.ec.europa.eu/latest-updates/eudamed-four-first-modules-will-be-mandatory-use-28-may-2026-2025-11-27_en . Note: my fetch of that page did not show a separate grace period for UDI/device registration; confirm the exact transitional wording in the Decision itself before quoting it externally.
2. MDR legacy-device transition (Regulation (EU) 2023/607): end dates 31 Dec 2027 for Class III and Class IIb implantable devices; 31 Dec 2028 for other IIb, IIa and sterile/measuring Class I; conditions included NB application by 26 May 2024 and written NB agreement by 26 Sep 2024. Source: https://www.noerr.com/en/insights/new-transitional-periods-for-legacy-devices-under-the-eu-medical-devices-regulation (law-firm summary; verify against the Regulation on EUR-Lex before external use). Implication: as of today about 15 months remain for the 2027 cohort, so certificate, UDI and label/IFU alignment work is concentrated now.
3. Buying signals to check per account (NOT yet collected in the CSV): open RA / regulatory-operations / EUDAMED roles on the company careers page, recent NB certificate news, new UDI-DI registration campaigns, labelling changes.

## Buyer roles
- Head of Regulatory Affairs: economic buyer and scope owner.
- PRRC (Art. 15 MDR): cares about documented conformity of records; useful champion, rarely budget holder.
- RA operations / regulatory data manager: day-to-day user; best pilot sponsor.
- QA / QMS manager: owns controlled documents and CAPA linkage.
Use role titles only; no named individuals are in this pack. Add people only from the company's own public pages, and never guess emails.

## Disqualifiers
- Danish-domiciled organisations (never cold-contact).
- Distributors/importers with no legal-manufacturer responsibility (offer fit weak).
- Pure pharma or consumer products with no MDR/IVDR device portfolio.
- Very small portfolio (under about 20 UDI-DIs) or no in-house RA function.
- Buyer expects clinical, legal or compliance opinions, or certification advice; ClinicOps returns exceptions for qualified reviewers to decide.
- Existing vendor lock-in for a full RIM/EUDAMED automation program in progress (park).
