# ClinicOps Global Distribution Playbook

Status: internal commercial operating asset. No private prospect data belongs in this file.

## Objective

Create global demand from medical-device manufacturers and regulatory partners that place or support devices on the EU market. The immediate target is **99 net-new qualified account opportunities** across 33 bounded sales cells.

The system optimizes for buyer movement, not activity volume.

## Commercial thesis

ClinicOps is most credible where regulated information must stay consistent across EUDAMED, product master/RIM, IFU, SS(C)P, labels/artwork and other controlled records. The entry service is a bounded regulatory integrity review; recurring value may emerge from change propagation and evidence-control operations.

This remains a commercial hypothesis until buyer evidence reaches E5/E6+.

## Sales-management rules

1. Every account must be net-new by default. Previous account contact suppresses further cold outreach unless an inbound response or separately approved follow-up justifies reopening it.
2. Before any outbound route is used, check the private outreach master plus live Sent Items in Outlook and Gmail.
3. Permanent do-not-contact: Ergomed Group and PrimeVigilance.
4. Danish-domiciled organisations are never cold-emailed.
5. Maximum 15 sends per day across the company.
6. A bounce is route-quality evidence, not buyer rejection.
7. No E4+ stage without a private evidence reference.
8. Do not use regulatory urgency to manufacture fear or imply non-compliance.
9. Do not claim end-to-end EUDAMED coverage, automated compliance determination, notified-body endorsement or legal assurance.
10. Kill low-information channels after repeated delivery with no buyer movement; reallocate to higher-signal routes.

Executable gate:

```bash
clinicops-sales-swarm sales/global-sales-swarm.json /private/path/net-new-leads.json
```

A lead is ineligible unless the master log, Outlook Sent Items and Gmail Sent Items were checked, the route is verified and the account was not previously contacted.

## 33-cell model

The canonical account owners remain the existing ClinicOps agents. The 33 sales cells are temporary specialist workers, not 33 permanent autonomous identities.

- **Sales Manager / Revenue:** Opportunity Architect owns prioritisation, offer and E6 conversion.
- **Prospecting / Discovery:** Customer Discovery Agent owns account research, outreach evidence and E4/E5 handoff.
- **Distribution:** Visibility Architect owns directories, communities, events, search surfaces and reusable inbound assets.
- **Regulatory Affairs review:** Regulatory Evidence Steward checks regulatory facts, role distinctions and evidence boundaries.
- **Quality / Lead-auditor review:** Release Sentinel checks traceability, claim discipline, reproducibility and control failures.
- **Delivery:** Portfolio Operator receives only activated work.

Each cell has one target: three net-new qualified accounts or partner routes. If a cell cannot produce them with evidence, the cell is changed or killed rather than padded with weak names.

## Global channel stack

### Search and machine discovery

Maintain a technically clean site, sitemap, robots file, structured data and concise machine-readable service summary. Publish only pages that answer distinct buyer questions; do not generate thin geographic SEO pages.

### Professional communities

Prioritise medical-device RA/QA communities, professional associations, technical education programmes and events where the audience already owns regulatory or quality work. Useful contribution beats promotional posting.

### Conferences and event ecosystems

Use current programmes to identify speakers, sponsors, vendors and attending companies with demonstrated RA/QA investment. Examples of current 2026 surfaces include European Medical Device & Diagnostics QARA, TOPRA activities, RAPS/MedTech events and regional regulatory summits. Event presence is a targeting and partnership signal; it is not buyer validation.

### Partner distribution

Authorised representatives, regulatory consultancies, QMS/RIM/UDI vendors and medical-device language-service providers can distribute ClinicOps into workflows they already serve. Partnership outreach should ask about a repeated workflow, not pitch a generic AI platform.

### Direct manufacturer outreach

Use only net-new accounts with a verified route and an evidence-backed reason the workflow may be relevant. Prefer one specific operational question over broad service menus.

### Durable directories / marketplaces

Prepare listings for credible medical-device professional directories and marketplaces only when account creation is approved. Listings should point to one useful free tool and one bounded review workflow.

## Offer path

1. Free browser-local integrity check or workload calculator.
2. Bounded human-reviewed regulatory integrity review.
3. Remediation / reconciliation work when explicitly scoped and activated.
4. Recurring Regulatory Change Operations only after repeated need and economic value are proven.

## Discovery questions

Use these to find pain instead of forcing a product:

- When an approved regulated fact changes, how do you know every affected system/document was updated?
- Which source is treated as authoritative when RIM/master, EUDAMED and controlled documents disagree?
- Where does the team spend the most time re-checking or tracing version authority?
- What downstream failure or delay occurs when a change is missed?
- Who owns the decision and who pays for the rework?
- Would a bounded review of one real change event be useful enough to fund or procure?

## Outcome scorecard

Weekly commercial reporting must show:

- net-new accounts qualified;
- routes verified;
- delivered demand tests;
- buyer responses (E4);
- repeated pain confirmations (E5);
- priced scope/procurement/budget-owner movement (E6);
- activated work (E7);
- accepted delivery/payment (E8);
- expansion/referral (E9);
- suppressed duplicates caught before outreach;
- bounces and route failures;
- channels killed or reallocated.

Impressions, page count, generated drafts and agent activity are diagnostic metrics only.
