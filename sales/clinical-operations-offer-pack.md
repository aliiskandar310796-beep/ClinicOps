# ClinicOps clinical-operations offer pack

Status: COMMERCIAL TEST MATERIAL — EU medical-device regulatory/change integrity is the current operating beachhead. The adjacent offers below are bounded demand tests until external evidence meets the promotion gates.

## Core commercial promise

ClinicOps helps clinical teams control operational change across evidence, records, systems and human review.

A ClinicOps review does not replace a sponsor, investigator, RA/QA owner, laboratory professional, clinician, privacy officer or other qualified decision-maker. It reduces the reconciliation burden around their decisions.

## Offer 1 — Trial Change Integrity Review

### Best-fit buyer

- sponsor clinical operations;
- CRO clinical operations / quality;
- study start-up / trial management;
- TMF / study-document operations;
- small-to-mid biotech teams coordinating vendors and sites.

### Trigger

A protocol amendment, approved study change or operational decision has occurred and the team needs evidence that the declared affected surfaces were updated, reviewed or explicitly resolved.

### Bounded scope

- one change event;
- 3–10 declared downstream surfaces;
- client-designated approved source;
- evidence references supplied or retrieved from agreed systems/sources;
- one named decision owner;
- one reviewer-ready evidence/discrepancy packet.

### Example surfaces

- protocol / amendment record;
- CTIS or submission record where in scope;
- participant-facing materials;
- site communication;
- training evidence;
- vendor/system configuration;
- study plans/manuals;
- implementation/closure record.

### Deliverable

- source/scope register;
- observation matrix;
- deterministic comparison states where appropriate;
- evidence-gap queue;
- owner routing;
- human-decision boundary;
- closure checklist;
- portable evidence packet.

### Recurring expansion

If the first review reveals repeated change events, test a monthly or per-amendment change-integrity service. Do not default to a subscription before cadence is confirmed.

## Shared qualification questions

1. What changed, and what record or decision proves it was approved?
2. Which downstream records, systems, vendors or people should reflect the change?
3. Which part of the reconciliation is repeated rather than exceptional?
4. What happens when a change is missed, late or difficult to reconstruct?
5. Who owns the final decision when sources conflict?
6. How often does this workflow occur?
7. What does the team use today to prove closure?
8. Is there a current project, inspection, study milestone or governance event creating urgency?
9. Who owns budget or procurement for solving it?
10. Would the buyer sponsor one bounded paid review on a real change event?

### TrialOps

Subject: One protocol amendment, every affected surface

ClinicOps is testing a bounded clinical-operations review for teams managing protocol and study changes. The review anchors one approved change, maps the records/systems/sites expected to reflect it, and returns an evidence-backed discrepancy and reviewer queue. It does not make GCP or sponsor decisions.

If amendment propagation or reconstructing closure is a repeated burden in your team, I would value 20 minutes to compare the workflow with what you do today.

### QualityOps

Subject: One QMS change, every controlled surface

ClinicOps is testing a bounded evidence-control review around SOP, CAPA and quality-system changes. The method anchors an approved source, checks only the downstream surfaces you declare in scope, exposes evidence/ownership gaps and hands the decision queue back to QA/RA.

If change propagation across documents, training, suppliers or systems is a recurring reconciliation problem, I would value a short workflow comparison.

### Clinical AI Ops

Subject: Evidence control around clinical AI changes

ClinicOps is testing a clinical-AI operations review that creates one reviewable register around use case, owner, vendor/system/version evidence, change history, human oversight and unresolved questions. It is deliberately not an automated compliance or clinical-safety verdict.

If your team is already maintaining this evidence across spreadsheets, policies and vendor files, I would value a short comparison of the current workflow.
