# ClinicOps live commercial offer pack

Status: LIVE SALES MATERIAL. Operational availability and commercial proof are tracked separately. These offers may be sold and delivered now within their stated boundaries; no offer implies a compliance, legal, clinical, safety, quality or release determination by ClinicOps.

## Core promise

ClinicOps turns one real operational change into a reviewer-ready evidence packet: approved source, declared scope, observations, explicit gaps, accountable owners, human decisions and closure evidence.

Preferred conversion path:

`qualified workflow -> bounded paid review -> accepted packet -> recurring cadence -> reusable lower-touch product`

Do not sell generic AI automation or generic compliance consulting first. Sell a bounded evidence outcome around one real workflow.

## Commercial architecture

Every segment has three revenue horizons:

1. **Active now** — fixed-scope review or sprint.
2. **Recurring after cadence is confirmed** — monitoring, register maintenance, periodic review or retainer.
3. **Lower-touch after repeated delivery** — browser-local tool, template, packet builder, partner license or narrow subscription.

## 1. Regulatory Integrity Review

**Best-fit buyers:** medical-device manufacturers, authorised representatives, RA/QA teams, regulatory consultancies and distribution partners.

**Trigger:** a regulatory, product, market, actor, evidence or document change needs to be reconciled across declared downstream surfaces.

**Bounded entry:** one change event or one defined evidence population; client-designated approved sources; 3–10 downstream surfaces; named decision owner; evidence/discrepancy packet.

**Recurring path:** regulatory-data/document lifecycle monitoring, evidence-register maintenance, periodic integrity review.

**Lower-touch path:** browser-local integrity checks, change packets, identifier/readiness tools and partner licenses.

## 2. Trial Change Integrity Review

**Best-fit buyers:** sponsor clinical operations, CRO operations/quality, study start-up, TMF/document operations and biotech study teams.

**Trigger:** a protocol amendment or approved study change must propagate across sites, records, vendors, systems, training or participant-facing material.

**Bounded entry:** one approved change; 3–10 declared downstream surfaces; named sponsor/operational decision owner; reviewer-ready propagation and closure packet.

**Recurring path:** per-amendment review, study evidence register, periodic site/study evidence reconciliation.

**Lower-touch path:** amendment impact mapper, study evidence packet builder, training/acknowledgement tracker.

## 3. QMS Change Integrity Review

**Best-fit buyers:** medical-device quality systems, supplier quality, quality engineering, document control and RA/QA partners.

**Trigger:** SOP, CAPA, supplier, training or quality-system change needs evidence of downstream propagation.

**Bounded entry:** one approved quality change; 3–10 affected controlled surfaces; named quality decision owner; evidence/closure packet.

**Recurring path:** QMS drift review, supplier evidence maintenance, periodic SOP/training/version reconciliation.

**Lower-touch path:** QMS change packet, controlled-document propagation checker, supplier evidence register.

## 4. Clinical AI Evidence Review

**Best-fit buyers:** healthcare digital leadership, clinical AI governance teams, digital-health/medtech operations, healthcare quality/risk teams and AI vendors serving clinical workflows.

**Trigger:** an AI system, vendor, version, intended workflow or oversight control changes and the evidence is fragmented across policies, vendor files and local records.

**Bounded entry:** 1–5 systems/use cases; declared operational purpose; supplied vendor/system/version evidence; named operational and decision owners; oversight/change register.

**Recurring path:** AI system register maintenance, vendor/model change review, periodic governance evidence packet.

**Lower-touch path:** AI change register, vendor evidence packet builder, human-oversight mapping template.

**Boundary:** not automatic AI Act compliance, clinical validation, cybersecurity certification, privacy compliance or medical-device classification.

## 5. Lab Change Integrity Review

**Best-fit buyers:** diagnostic laboratories, POCT programs, lab quality managers, equipment/service operators and accreditation support partners.

**Trigger:** method, equipment, software, SOP, competency or QC-related change must be reconciled across the declared operational evidence set.

**Bounded entry:** one change event; approved method/SOP/equipment source; 3–10 affected records or controls; named laboratory decision owner; review packet.

**Recurring path:** equipment/service evidence register, competency/change tracking, periodic evidence-completeness review.

**Lower-touch path:** lab change packet, competency/version tracker, equipment evidence register template.

## 6. Clinic Operations Evidence Review

**Best-fit buyers:** clinics, care-delivery groups, outpatient operators, digital clinics and vendors supporting clinical workflows.

**Trigger:** vendor, SOP, training, workflow or incident-response change needs a traceable operational record across people, systems and evidence.

**Bounded entry:** one operational change; declared approved policy/SOP/vendor decision; 3–10 affected surfaces; named clinic owner; evidence and unresolved-action packet.

**Recurring path:** vendor evidence renewal, SOP/training checks, periodic operational evidence-health review.

**Lower-touch path:** vendor/SOP packet builder, evidence register, renewal/change checklist.

**Boundary:** jurisdiction-specific qualified owners retain clinical, legal, privacy and regulatory decisions.

## 7. Controlled Medical Content Change Review

**Best-fit buyers:** manufacturers, authorised representatives, localization partners, distributors and controlled-content operations teams.

**Trigger:** approved source content changes and downstream market-language or partner-controlled versions need traceable propagation.

**Bounded entry:** one approved content change; 3–10 controlled downstream versions/surfaces; source/version provenance; named content/regulatory owner; discrepancy packet.

**Recurring path:** version/terminology register, periodic multilingual evidence review, partner/distributor change packets.

**Lower-touch path:** controlled-content diff packet, version register, partner-ready evidence template.

**Boundary:** ClinicOps does not become a generic translation agency; the value is controlled change and evidence integrity.

## Qualification questions used across every fleet

1. What changed, and what record proves approval?
2. Which downstream records, systems, vendors, sites or people should reflect it?
3. Which part of the reconciliation repeats?
4. What happens when the change is missed, late or hard to reconstruct?
5. Who owns the final decision when sources conflict?
6. How often does the workflow occur?
7. What evidence proves closure today?
8. What current milestone or event creates urgency?
9. Who owns budget or procurement?
10. Would the buyer sponsor one bounded paid review on a real event?

## Distribution rule

Each segment must maintain at least three active acquisition routes and should not derive more than half of planned activity from one channel when alternatives exist. Preferred routes are:

- warm introductions and partner referrals;
- browser-local tools and scope-brief conversion;
- research/content distribution;
- professional communities/events;
- existing relationship follow-up;
- bounded cold outreach only when the company-wide outbound controller permits it.

## Pricing posture

Lead with fixed scope rather than hourly consulting. Price should reflect the bounded evidence outcome, number of declared surfaces, evidence readiness, turnaround urgency and review complexity. Do not promise recurring pricing until cadence is real.

## Productization gate

A lower-touch product graduates only when at least two independent real deliveries expose substantially the same object model, review states, human boundary and buyer outcome. Productization should reduce reconciliation cost, not invent demand.
