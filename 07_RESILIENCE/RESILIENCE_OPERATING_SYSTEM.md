# ClinicOps Resilience Operating System

## Goal
Build a company that adapts faster than the environment changes.

## Weekly review questions

- What changed in regulation?
- What changed in customer needs?
- What new risk appeared?
- What assumption should be tested?
- What asset can be created from learning?

## Resilience layers

### Knowledge
Own structured regulatory intelligence.

### Distribution
Maintain website, research, partnerships and audience.

### Revenue
Maintain multiple engines:
- assessments
- subscriptions
- digital products
- training
- intelligence products

### Technology
Automate repeatable work while preserving quality.

## Rule
Every failure becomes documented learning.
