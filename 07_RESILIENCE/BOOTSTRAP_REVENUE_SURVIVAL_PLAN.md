# ClinicOps Bootstrap Revenue Survival Plan

## Objective

Build a resilient regulatory intelligence company while operating under severe capital constraints.

The constraint is a design advantage: ClinicOps must become revenue-funded, efficient and adaptive.

## Core rule

No expensive platform before customer validation.

Use:

- GitHub as operating system
- AI as leverage
- clinicops.dk as distribution hub
- email as relationship channel
- research as authority engine
- human regulatory judgement as premium value

## First 12-month mission

Reach enough revenue to reinvest.

Priority order:

1. First paying customers
2. Repeatable delivery
3. Recurring revenue
4. Automation
5. Platform investment

## Revenue engines

### Engine 1: Intelligence assessments

Primary cash generator.

Products:

- Transition Intelligence Scan
- EUDAMED/SS(C)P reconciliation review
- Portfolio transition assessment

Goal:
Create immediate customer value with minimal software.

### Engine 2: Monitoring subscriptions

Convert projects into recurring revenue.

Examples:

- regulatory signal monitoring
- portfolio updates
- document intelligence

### Engine 3: Digital products

Create scalable assets:

- templates
- checklists
- workflows
- training material

### Engine 4: Research authority

Turn validated research into:

- SEO content
- LinkedIn authority
- partner conversations
- inbound opportunities

## Weekly operating loop

Every week:

### Build one asset

Example:

- tool
- research note
- checklist
- report template

### Run one market experiment

Example:

- customer conversation
- landing page test
- partner outreach

### Improve one system

Example:

- automation
- validation
- delivery speed

## Cash discipline

Before spending money ask:

1. Does this create revenue?
2. Does this reduce delivery time?
3. Does this create proprietary knowledge?
4. Does this improve customer trust?

If no, defer.

## Long-term vision

ClinicOps becomes the regulatory intelligence layer for EU medical device operations:

Signals -> Evidence -> Intelligence -> Decisions -> Continuous monitoring.

The company scales through knowledge compounding, not excessive fixed costs.
