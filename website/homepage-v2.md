# ClinicOps homepage — canonical commercial story

Status: production source of truth.

## Positioning

**Medical Device Regulatory Operations**

Focused on regulatory data, controlled documents and lifecycle change for teams supporting the EU market.

## Hero

### When a regulated fact changes, know what else must change.

ClinicOps helps medical-device manufacturers, authorised representatives and regulatory partners keep regulatory data, controlled documents and lifecycle changes aligned across the surfaces they put in scope.

We reconcile client-approved controlled sources against downstream records such as EUDAMED data, RIM/master data, IFU, SS(C)P, labels and artwork; preserve evidence; and route unresolved authority to the right human decision owner instead of guessing.

Primary CTA: **Try the free integrity check**

Secondary CTA: **See the specimen register**

Commercial CTA: **Scope a real review**

## Core operating problem

A regulatory change rarely lives in one place. The costly failure mode is drift between sources, systems and documents: one value changes, another remains stale, two controlled sources disagree, or the team later has to reconstruct why a value was accepted.

Formal finding state when supplied controlled sources conflict: **AUTHORITATIVE SOURCE UNRESOLVED**. ClinicOps never silently chooses the winning value.

## Commercial flow

### 1. Regulatory Integrity Review

Find discrepancies, missing values, stale downstream records and unresolved source authority. Deliver a human-reviewed evidence and discrepancy register.

### 2. Regulatory Information Remediation

After client RA/QA approves authoritative values, coordinate agreed corrections across the records and documents placed in scope.

### 3. Regulatory Change Operations

For recurring change, identify impacted records, map affected surfaces, coordinate implementation, verify propagation and document closure.

The commercial story is therefore:

**Review → Remediate → Maintain**

## Proof ladder

Visitors should be able to move through the site in this order:

1. understand the problem;
2. test one value privately with the browser-local integrity check;
3. inspect the synthetic specimen register to understand the deliverable;
4. quantify current review burden if useful;
5. build a browser-local scope brief;
6. run one bounded live review;
7. remediate confirmed discrepancies after client RA/QA decisions;
8. expand into recurring Regulatory Change Operations only when repeated need is proven.

## Typical first live review

- one device family / Basic UDI-DI group;
- 1–3 client-approved controlled sources;
- 2–6 downstream surfaces;
- 3–10 declared fields;
- optional approved change event;
- one named qualified reviewer.

## Best fit

- manufacturers worldwide maintaining devices on the EU market;
- authorised representatives reconciling evidence across manufacturer portfolios;
- regulatory and quality partners that need an evidence-control layer around expert review and change work.

## Boundaries

- no automated legal or compliance determination;
- no automatic authoritative-source decision when supplied sources conflict;
- no complete-register claim from bounded public probes;
- no compliance allegation from a missing public record alone;
- no release authorization from a ClinicOps work product;
- client RA/QA retains authoritative-source and release decisions.
