## Intro

Moving a portfolio from fragmented public records and internal evidence to an actionable transition queue is not the same as running a compliance check.

ClinicOps helps manufacturers, authorised representatives and regulatory partners reconcile portfolio evidence, separate legacy and MDR registration paths, surface unresolved inputs and decide what deserves human review first.

**Primary CTA:** Request a portfolio transition map

## What the review can organise

- manufacturer and actor-role context;
- legacy versus MDR registration path;
- identifier reconciliation questions;
- certificate or transition timing where reliable evidence is supplied;
- SS(C)P workflow questions where applicable;
- unresolved evidence gaps;
- a prioritised human-review queue.

## Legacy identifier screening

For EUDAMED legacy-device identification, the EUDAMED DI uses the B- prefix and stands in place of a Basic UDI-DI for the legacy registration model. Identifier structure alone does not establish the complete regulatory or SS(C)P state of a device. [CO-CLM-0004]

ClinicOps therefore uses identifier structure as a screening input, not as a standalone compliance conclusion.

## Designed for portfolio operations

The Transition Map is especially useful when the operational problem is portfolio-scale reconciliation rather than a single-record question.

Best-fit users include:

- authorised representatives managing multiple manufacturers;
- manufacturers with class III or implantable transition workload;
- regulatory consultancies that want a repeatable evidence and prioritisation layer.

## Deliverable

A typical pilot produces:

1. intake diagnostics;
2. role-aware portfolio segmentation;
3. priority work queue;
4. evidence-gap ledger;
5. human-reviewed action plan.

The deliverable is a work plan, not an allegation that a manufacturer is non-compliant.

## Start small

Send a small portfolio export or discuss the workflow first. ClinicOps will identify what can be screened reproducibly and where manufacturer-controlled or other authoritative evidence is still required.
