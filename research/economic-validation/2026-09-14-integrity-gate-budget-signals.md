# Integrity Gate economic plausibility — public budget signals

Date: 2026-09-14  
Status: **E1 supporting market evidence only — not buyer validation**

## Question

Is there current public evidence that medical-device companies allocate real budget/headcount to regulatory-data quality, EUDAMED/UDI operations, lifecycle change impact and traceability workflows adjacent to the Integrity Gate hypothesis?

## Observed signals

### Mandatory EUDAMED UDI/Device workload

The European Commission's UDI/Device registration page states that manufacturers must submit UDI/Device information for devices placed on the EU market and that the UDI/Devices module became mandatory to use from 28 May 2026.

Source: https://health.ec.europa.eu/medical-devices-eudamed/udidevice-registration_en

Interpretation: this creates a current operational data-maintenance surface. It does **not** prove that manufacturers need or will buy ClinicOps.

### Dedicated EUDAMED / UDI headcount

A current DePuy Synthes posting seeks a `Manager, EUDAMED & UDI` responsible for accurate, timely regulatory data submissions, cross-functional coordination and product-lifecycle support.

Source: https://notify.careers/postings/2cbe4142-1256-44f8-ac3c-7e7208dfbf65

A current regulatory UDI/data-syndication role describes responsibility across GUDID, EUDAMED and other databases, including data remediation, data quality, GTIN/product hierarchy governance and cross-functional work with PLM/RIM/MDM/IT/Regulatory/Quality.

Source: https://www.monster.com/job-openings/regulatory-udi-data-syndication-lead-eagan-mn--3eb91a6c-03c1-475e-88aa-ddb61c0ea86c

A current Medtronic Regulatory Information Management Analyst posting explicitly includes process improvement and data-quality improvement in the RIM environment.

Source: https://medtronic.wd1.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/MedtronicCareers/job/IT-Business-Analyst_R70427-1

Interpretation: manufacturers spend salary/headcount on adjacent data-governance work. This is category-budget evidence, not willingness-to-pay evidence for Integrity Gate.

### Change-impact work is a paid regulatory responsibility

Current medical-device regulatory postings explicitly assign change-impact analysis, labeling/document accuracy and regulatory impact ownership to experienced RA/compliance staff.

Sources:

- https://dornier-medtech.jobs.personio.de/job/2659468
- https://onehealthineers.wd3.myworkdayjobs.com/en-US/SHSJB/job/Senior-Regulatory-Affairs-Specialist_R-29562-1

Interpretation: change propagation/impact is real expert work. The commercial question is whether ClinicOps can reduce its cost/cycle time enough to earn a separate budget.

### Adjacent software/service budgets exist

Ketryx sells connected lifecycle/compliance software on a subscription model. Its paid Startup, Business and Enterprise plans require sales contact; its product material emphasizes traceability, change management, automated documentation and AI-assisted review.

Sources:

- https://www.ketryx.com/pricing
- https://www.ketryx.com/function/quality
- https://www.ketryx.com/capabilities/eqms

Dovetail QMS Plus publishes small/medium-business pricing from €300/month and enterprise pricing by quote; it also sells embedded regulatory services on fixed/milestone pricing.

Sources:

- https://www.withdovetail.com/products/platform
- https://www.withdovetail.com/products/envoy

Interpretation: buyers already allocate budget to adjacent controlled-document, traceability, QMS and regulatory-service workflows. This supports economic plausibility, not product-market fit for ClinicOps.

## What this evidence does and does not prove

It supports:

- a current regulatory-data/change-control workload exists;
- manufacturers allocate expert headcount to it;
- adjacent vendors capture recurring software and service budgets;
- an evidence-controlled pre-release/change-propagation workflow is economically plausible enough to test.

It does **not** support:

- a claim that Integrity Gate is commercially validated;
- a claim that any specific manufacturer has the problem;
- a claim that buyers prefer software over service;
- a specific price;
- a claim that ClinicOps reduces cost or risk in real customer use.

Those require E4–E9 evidence in the private economic-validation ledger.

## Next falsification target

The next evidence should answer a narrower question than “is the market big?”:

> Will a qualified buyer allocate a concrete next step, scope, procurement effort or money to have one real cross-system regulatory-information/change-propagation workflow checked?

Until that happens, keep Integrity Gate at `TEST`.
