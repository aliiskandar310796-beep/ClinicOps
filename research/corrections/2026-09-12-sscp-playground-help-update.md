# EUDAMED SS(C)P Playground help update — 12 September 2026

## Why this note exists

A fresh EUDAMED change-watch signal on 12 September 2026 pointed to Commission help content labelled **v3.31.2** and published on 7 September 2026. The update materially strengthens the evidence that the revised manufacturer-side SS(C)P workflow described in MDCG 2026-4 is represented in the Commission's current **Playground/help** material.

It does **not** establish that the planned Production rollout has already occurred.

This distinction is important because ClinicOps claim `CO-CLM-0008` describes the MDCG 2026-4 plan for an October 2026 Production deployment, while `CO-CLM-0009` describes the revised manufacturer/notified-body operating model. This note is supporting evidence for the operating model, not evidence that the Production timing has moved forward.

## What the current help material documents

The manufacturer-side workflow now explicitly documents that a manufacturer can:

1. create a new SS(C)P record;
2. select the applicable regulation and notified body;
3. identify one or more relevant Basic UDI-DIs;
4. provide the SS(C)P reference number, revision number, issue date and master-document language;
5. upload the SS(C)P master document and submit the record with confirmation concerning notified-body validation;
6. create a new SS(C)P version, including a new revision number, issue date and master document and, where applicable, changes to linked Basic UDI-DIs;
7. add one or multiple translation documents and submit them into the SS(C)P record.

The manufacturer management screen also exposes SS(C)P status values including **Validated**, **Not validated** and **Partially validated**.

## What this does not prove

Do not infer any of the following from these pages alone:

- that the revised SS(C)P workflow has already been deployed to EUDAMED Production;
- that the October 2026 Production timing in MDCG 2026-4 has been superseded;
- that every Production user currently has the described functions;
- that a missing public SS(C)P link establishes non-compliance;
- that public EUDAMED data is complete or suitable for a full-register compliance audit.

The `eudamed-play-help` hostname is itself a reason to preserve the Playground/Production distinction until a Production release note, Production help page, or directly verified Production behaviour establishes otherwise.

## ClinicOps operating consequence

Treat this as **primary evidence that the planned manufacturer-side SS(C)P operating model is concretely represented in the Commission's current Playground/help documentation**.

Until Production deployment is independently verified:

- keep `CO-CLM-0008` qualified as a planned Production deployment;
- keep `CO-CLM-0009` qualified and do not rewrite it as a statement of current Production behaviour;
- use this note internally and in research reasoning to support the existence and shape of the manufacturer-side workflow;
- do not publish a new external claim saying the October functionality is already live.
