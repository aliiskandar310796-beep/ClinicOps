# MDCG 2026-4 rev.1: Production date and legacy-upload date changed — 14 November 2026

## What changed
The document at the Commission URL used by claims CO-CLM-0005, 0008, 0009 and 0010 is now **MDCG 2026-4 rev.1, dated October 2026**. Read at the primary source on 14 November 2026:

- Section 3, footnote 2 (page 2): Playground deployment planned July 2026; **Production deployment December 2026**. The June version said October 2026.
- Section 4 (page 3): manufacturers **should** upload SS(C)Ps for devices placed on the market before mandatory use as soon as possible and **no later than 27 March 2027**. The June version said 27 February 2027.
- Section 2 (page 2): manufacturer uploads master version and translations; notified body indicates the validated SS(C)P by ticking the box(es) for the relevant Basic UDI-DI(s). Unchanged in substance.
- The document carries no revision-history note. The change is established by comparison with the June version and a secondary report (Pure Global, 1 November 2026) of the Commission's 30 October 2026 announcement; the primary document gives the new dates.
