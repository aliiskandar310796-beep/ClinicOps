# 2026-09-08 — class III headline reversal

## Rejected headline
Do not state or imply that MDR class III manufacturer registrations generally lack linked SS(C)Ps.

## Corrected observation
In the systematic sample described by ClinicOps on 8 September 2026: 95 pages, 4,750 UDI-DI records, 213 class III rows, 98 distinct class III records. The reported strata were 70 legacy registrations, 11 system/procedure-pack records, and 17 MDR manufacturer registrations. All 17 sampled MDR manufacturer registrations had linked SS(C)P metadata and were reported as validated.

## Commercial interpretation
Treat the observed opportunity as a **legacy-to-MDR transition and document-operations backlog**, not a diligence-failure story.

## Standing qualifications
- The sample is not a full-register census.
- Deep-offset public API reachability was observed to fail above roughly page 30,000; this must remain a measured limitation unless re-tested.
- `pageSize` was observed to be capped at 50; re-test before relying on it.
- A B-prefix/legacy-to-no-linked-SS(C)P consequence is a ClinicOps derivation/observed system behavior unless supported by a source that states that exact consequence.
- Procedure-pack risk class requires role-aware interpretation.
