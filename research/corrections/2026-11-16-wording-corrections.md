# Wording corrections on the public pages — 16 November 2026

A review of the public site on 16 November 2026 found statements that implied more capacity, cover or certainty than ClinicOps has, one internal contradiction, one dangling reference and two points where the privacy notice did not match practice. Each is a wording correction, not a change in what ClinicOps does. Each entry below states what was wrong, what changed and which pages were affected.

## Correction 1: capacity-implying statements removed

**What was wrong.** "Specialist depth on demand ... across medicine and nursing" (About), "independent specialist review — medicine and nursing" (Medical devices page), "independent subject-matter expert whose credentials are checked" (Denmark page), "a private, project-specific expert-network model" and "the network is a controlled delivery model" (Partners page), "Qualified Danish pharmacovigilance capability ... a safety-operations resource that works as an extension of your QPPV system ... contract-ready for the 2026 rules", "set up for exactly that: one named, auditable site your PSMF can list", "Governed, audit-ready method", and "Danish linguistic validation" (Contact, Use cases). Together these read as a standing team, a staffed network or a ready-made compliance arrangement. None exists: ClinicOps is one person, specialist enquiries are paused, and no specialist is named on the site.

**What changed.** The wording now says ClinicOps does the work itself and approaches a specialist for one defined question only, with the client's approval beforehand; that there is no standing team; and that no specialty or country is permanently staffed. The Danish pharmacovigilance section is described as language support scoped in writing. "Linguistic validation" is no longer offered as a service.

**Pages.** About, Medical devices, Contact, Use cases, Denmark market access, Danish DHPC checker, Working with partners, What a pilot looks like, privacy notice, Specialist expression of interest (paused).

## Correction 2: the Partners page contradicted itself

**What was wrong.** One line said white-label work is delivered in the partner's format for the partner to put its name on; another said "the ClinicOps engagement lead signs every deliverable". A partner could not tell whether a ClinicOps signature appears in what the end client sees.

**What changed.** Two documents are now described separately. The handover note, signed by ClinicOps, states what was done, by whom and what was not, and stays between the two parties. The work product is white-label where the partner agrees that in writing, and then carries no ClinicOps signature. The page also gained partner types, a "Not in scope" list, a notified-body conflict check, a data default, a statement that work without a written limit of liability is not offered, and a section for language-service providers with the scope boundary.

**Registry action.** New claim `CO-CLM-0070` (MDR Annex VII points 1.2.3 and 1.2.4, qualified). The points were read twice, on EUR-Lex and on legislation.gov.uk, through a page summariser; re-read the Official Journal text before any wording that quotes them.

## Correction 3: "Danish-led" replaced

**What was wrong.** The Denmark page said ClinicOps is "Danish-led". The practice is not led from Denmark and the positioning is EU-wide with a Denmark specialism.

**What changed.** "Denmark-specialised and EU-wide". The old phrase is now on the retired-strings list.

**Pages.** Denmark market access.

## Correction 4: privacy notice

**What was wrong.** Section 5 (and its Danish counterpart, section 9) listed "a regulatory or vigilance contact" among the roles processed for outreach, although ClinicOps does not write to vigilance mailboxes. It said "we keep no marketing list" without saying that prospect and suppression records exist. Section 6 named no AI service although ClinicOps uses AI assistants to help draft text. It also referred to an expression-of-interest builder that no longer exists.

**What changed.** "Vigilance contact" removed in both languages. The list statement now reads: no newsletter or mailing list, only the prospect and suppression records described in the same section. An AI-assistants line was added to section 6 (and in Danish): enquiry content, personal data and client files are not entered into AI tools unless the client agrees in writing. The stale builder reference was removed. The sentence "address and tax ID on request" was not touched.

**Pages.** Privacy notice.

## Correction 5: dangling reference

**What was wrong.** The home page said the Danish work stays "within the limits stated at the foot of this page". The footer states no such limits.

**What changed.** The limits are now stated in the sentence itself.

**Pages.** Home.

## Correction 6: SS(C)P operations page rewritten

**What was wrong.** The page was written as a sales page ("what ClinicOps can organise", "good fit", two calls to action) and carried no date, although MDCG 2026-4 rev.1 had moved the Production plan.

**What changed.** It is now a plain explainer: what an SS(C)P is, who does what, the two dates from MDCG 2026-4 rev.1 (December 2026 plan, 27 March 2027 recommendation) with a "rules as of" line, and a boundary line. The page title changed with it.

**Registry action.** `CO-CLM-0019` gained `website` in its allowed uses, after the MDCG 2026-4 rev.1 document was read again on 16 November 2026 (December 2026 Production plan, "no later than 27 March 2027", manufacturer and notified-body roles all confirmed). Its limitation, that the date is a recommendation and not a statutory deadline, is stated on the page.

**Pages.** SS(C)P operations.

## Correction 7: About did not say ClinicOps is one person, and covered AI only for the tools

**What was wrong.** The About page never said that ClinicOps is one self-employed person, which a buyer would read as a firm. It covered AI only for the free tools, so a reader could infer that site text and research notes are not AI-assisted. They are. The September 2026 SS(C)P scan states that language-model agents read the public records.

**What changed.** A plain one-person statement (no certification held, no insurance cover claimed), an AI-assisted drafting paragraph, a corrections protocol (acknowledged within 2 working days, decided within 10, "under review" flag on challenged pages) and three working rules (no referral fees, no sponsored research, findings are never a sales lever) were added.

**Pages.** About.
