How a project runs

  1. Agree the scope

    One page: what is in, what is out, what done looks like, and who on your side decides.

  2. Work in short stages

    At the end of each stage you see what exists, what is still open and what carrying on costs. You can stop there.

  3. Keep one log

    Risks, open questions, changes and decisions in one list, each with an owner and a date.

  4. Hand over

    Files, the log and the evidence behind every closed item, so your team or another consultant can carry on without ClinicOps.

The stages follow PRINCE2, and the eCOA workbench is organised the same way. PRINCE2® is a registered trademark of PeopleCert.

How ClinicOps is run

ClinicOps is a small practice, not a platform or a firm. Every project has one accountable owner, a written scope, and files and a log you keep. How ClinicOps works →

Work is performed within demonstrated competence and agreed scope. Formal statutory roles — QPPV and deputies, PRRC, authorised-representative duties, conformity decisions — are held only where explicitly appointed, and medical and regulatory sign-off stays with the formally responsible person or organisation.

Before you write

What does it cost?
ClinicOps quotes after a written scope brief, so you see the price before any work starts. The free tools stay free and need no sign-up.
How long does it take?
Timing is agreed in writing at scope. It pauses while evidence is outstanding, so complete records keep it short.
What happens to my data?
The tools run in your browser and send nothing anywhere. For project work, please do not send patient-identifiable information. The contact page says how to share records.
What if the person on my project is unavailable?
Work runs in short stages, and each ends with files and a log that someone else can pick up. If a question needs another specialist, ClinicOps says so and involves one only by agreement with you.
What do you need from me first?
What changed or what the question is, which records or systems it touches, and who on your side decides. That is enough to start.

Have a project in mind?

Write to info@clinicops.dk or fill in the short scope form. A few lines about the study or the product is enough to start.
