SS(C)P operations explained: what it is and who does what

An SS(C)P is a manufacturer’s summary of safety and clinical performance. This page says what it is, who does which part, which dates apply, and where ClinicOps stops. It covers MDR Article 32 only.

Rules as of 14 November 2026 · last verified against the sources named below · next review by 14 December 2026.

What it is

“SS(C)P” is the shorthand used here for the summary of safety and clinical performance. Under MDR Article 32(1), the manufacturer draws up the summary for implantable devices and class III devices, subject to the Article 32 exclusions. The summary must include the identification of the device, including the Basic UDI-DI (Article 32(2)(a)).

Who does what

Manufacturer

Draws up the SS(C)P. Under the operating model in MDCG 2026-4, uploads the master version and its translations to EUDAMED.

Notified body

Under the same model, indicates which SS(C)P is validated by ticking the box for the relevant Basic UDI-DI(s).

ClinicOps

None of these roles. On request, ClinicOps compares the records that describe the same device and lists where they differ.

MDCG 2026-4 is a non-binding position paper tied to the new EUDAMED functionality. Check current Production behaviour before relying on the model operationally. Other roles, such as authorised representatives and importers, are not covered on this page.

Dates

Both dates come from MDCG 2026-4 rev.1 (October 2026). The first is a plan for the revised model; the second is a recommendation, not a statutory deadline.

  • December 2026. Planned Production deployment in EUDAMED of the revised SS(C)P operating model, after a planned Playground deployment in July 2026. These are plans, not evidence that the functionality is available.
  • 27 March 2027. For devices placed on the market before mandatory use of the UDI/Devices module, manufacturers “should” upload SS(C)Ps as soon as possible and no later than this date, with manufacturers and notified bodies aligning their timelines.

Source: MDCG 2026-4 rev.1, European Commission. Plans can change; check the Commission’s current status. The rules-as-of date and next review are in the stamp under the heading and in the Last verified table.

Boundary

ClinicOps does not draw up, validate or approve an SS(C)P, holds no manufacturer, authorised-representative or notified-body role, and does not decide whether a record is correct. A missing public field is not a finding of non-compliance. Where records differ, ClinicOps lists the difference for the responsible person to resolve.
