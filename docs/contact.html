Not ready to scope anything? Ask a question.

You do not need to name your company, product or study. Describe the situation as a pattern (“a class IIb manufacturer whose legal manufacturer address changed”, “a sponsor adding a Danish version of a patient diary”) and say what you want to know. Your email address shows who you are, so write from an address you are comfortable with.

Send it to support@clinicops.dk, or use the pre-filled email above. If a public source answers it, the reply names the source. If it needs your records, the reply says what a scope would look like, and you decide. ClinicOps does not forward your question or add you to a mailing list. Scope requests go to info@clinicops.dk.

What makes scoping fast

The change or question

What changed, what is uncertain, or what Denmark/EU market-access question needs to be answered?

The evidence population

Which approved sources, systems, records, documents, languages or sites are affected?

The accountable owner

Who owns the regulatory, clinical, quality, legal, safety or release decision?

The desired closure

What would done look like for you? A list of evidence, a map of what the change touches, a list of who owns what, or corrected records?

Data handling

Do not send patient-identifiable information, confidential client files or unnecessary personal data by ordinary email before scope and secure handling are agreed. The scope brief and the browser-local tools send nothing to ClinicOps.

Who answers

Messages to info@clinicops.dk and support@clinicops.dk are read and answered by ClinicOps, which offers services to sponsors and device manufacturers in the EU.

Working with partners

Scope before authority. Taking part in a project does not make an expert, clinician or consultant the owner of a decision. Responsibility is set out in writing for each engagement.
