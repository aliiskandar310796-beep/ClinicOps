Before you write

Try the free tools first if useful: the EUDAMED Identifier Check screens a single identifier locally in your browser; the Transition Readiness Score gives a quick self-reported readiness signal. Neither sends anything to ClinicOps.

What to include

  • a short description of the portfolio or problem;
  • rough portfolio size and device classes involved, if known;
  • any existing evidence you can share (a small export is enough to start);
  • whether you are a manufacturer, authorised representative, or regulatory consultancy.

Do not send confidential client data by email until a scope and secure handling method are agreed.
