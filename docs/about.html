About ClinicOps

Danish-led. EU-wide. Built around accountable evidence.

ClinicOps operates between changing evidence and accountable human decisions across medical products, clinical research, healthcare technology and care-delivery operations.

What ClinicOps is

ClinicOps is an evidence-controlled operations practice. The work is deliberately structured: identify the authoritative or approved source, define scope, preserve provenance, expose uncertainty, route unresolved questions to the right owner and retain closure evidence.

Market access

Denmark-specific authority, language and evidence workflows connected to EU requirements.

Medical products

Regulatory integrity, MDR/IVDR, EUDAMED, quality and controlled-document change.

Clinical research

Trial and site change propagation, sponsor/CRO/site handoffs and evidence closure.

Clinical operations

AI, laboratories, clinics, controlled content, vendors, SOPs, competencies and recurring operational change.

Why Denmark matters

ClinicOps.dk is not a geographic decoration. Denmark is the local-market anchor: Danish authorities, Danish healthcare context, Danish-language requirements and Danish professional expertise can be brought into scope when relevant. The wider delivery model remains EU-wide and English-first.

Who does the work

ClinicOps is the specialist practice of Ali Iskandar — a native Danish speaker with an MSc in Medicine (Aalborg University) and 500+ regulated documentation projects across 6+ years in medical-device and life-science documentation, regulatory operations and pharmacovigilance environments. Engagements are delivered personally. Where a piece of work needs additional competence, a named independent specialist is brought in for that engagement and disclosed to the client — nothing is subcontracted silently. ClinicOps is openly building that specialist bench; independent experts can introduce themselves there.

Delivery record

This is not a practice waiting for its first work. More than 500 regulated documentation assignments have been delivered — from single-page labels to 60,000+ word SS(C)P reviews — for many of the world’s leading medical-device and pharmaceutical manufacturers, through global language-service providers, with a 100% on-time record. The professional background behind it spans regulatory consulting (Parexel), CRO clinical monitoring (Medpace, Further Group) and pharmacovigilance (PrimeVigilance/Ergomed), across orthopaedics, vascular intervention, IVD, imaging, ophthalmology, wound care and cardiac monitoring.

Background

ClinicOps was founded from a Danish medical and life-science perspective, with experience across regulatory consulting, clinical operations and pharmacovigilance environments. The company’s public method emphasizes source discipline, explicit uncertainty and accountable human review.

Authenticity over inflated authority

ClinicOps does not present every collaborator as an employee, every public-record signal as a finding, or every expert opinion as a regulatory or clinical conclusion. Named professional profiles are used only with consent and verifiable credentials. Project ownership is stated explicitly.

Professional boundary. ClinicOps provides consulting, evidence control and operational support. Patient-facing healthcare delivery, independent medical practice or other regulated professional activity is not assumed merely because a physician, nurse or other authorised professional participates in the network.
