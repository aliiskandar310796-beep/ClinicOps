About

Evidence-controlled clinical operations

ClinicOps is building the operating layer between changing evidence and accountable human decisions across clinical, medical-product and care-delivery workflows. EU medical-device regulatory integrity is the current commercial beachhead.

Why the name ClinicOps

The brand is intentionally broader than one regulation. ClinicOps should remain recognisably about the operations around clinics and clinical work: controlled records, study and site workflows, medical-product quality, diagnostic evidence, clinical technology, vendors, training, change and review.

That does not mean offering everything. New lanes are tested manually first and only become ClinicOps products when buyers demonstrate repeated operational pain and willingness to pay.

Medical-product operations

Regulatory and quality changes, controlled documents, lifecycle records and partner handoffs.

Clinical research operations

Study and site evidence, amendments, essential records, handoffs and accountable review.

Clinical technology operations

AI and other clinical systems: ownership, vendor evidence, change history, human-oversight boundaries and review packets.

Clinic and diagnostic operations

SOPs, vendor evidence, competencies, laboratory or point-of-care records and recurring change control.

Current commercial focus. ClinicOps is still proving its EU medical-device change-integrity offer. Adjacent clinical-operation lanes are discovery and validation work until real buyer evidence earns the right to expand.

Operating model

Capture the approved reference, record the observation, preserve provenance and role context, expose uncertainty, route unresolved questions to the accountable owner, and preserve the human decision in a reviewable evidence packet.

Machine findings are not legal, regulatory, clinical, safety or compliance determinations. ClinicOps is designed to make expert review more controlled and reproducible, not to replace the expert who owns the decision.

Background

Native Danish. MSc in Medicine, Aalborg University. Background across regulatory consulting, clinical operations and pharmacovigilance with CROs and life-sciences environments.

Danish base, international scope

ClinicOps.dk is Danish, but ClinicOps is not a Denmark-only service. The long-term scope is evidence-controlled clinical operations for teams working across regulated healthcare and life-sciences environments, with each offer bounded by the relevant jurisdiction and qualified human ownership.
