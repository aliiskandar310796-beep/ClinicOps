About

Founder-led EU medical device regulatory intelligence

ClinicOps is a Danish-based, EU-wide regulatory intelligence practice for MedTech — founded and operated by Ali Iskandar.

Operating model

Capture the signal, classify the evidence, preserve actor-role and duty-holder context, block unsupported claims, prioritise the work, deliver a human-reviewed action plan. Machine breadth, human judgement, evidence that survives review.

Public-register screening is an evidence input, not a compliance determination — the deliverable is a prioritised work plan, and regulatory sign-off stays with your RA/QA team.

Background

Native Danish. MSc in Medicine, Aalborg University. Background across regulatory consulting, clinical operations and pharmacovigilance with CROs and life-sciences environments.

Danish base, EU-wide scope

ClinicOps.dk is Danish, but ClinicOps is not positioned as a Denmark-only regulatory service. Danish-market document review is a focused local capability inside a broader EU MedTech regulatory-operations practice.
