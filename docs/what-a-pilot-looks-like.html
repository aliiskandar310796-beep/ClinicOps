What a pilot looks like.

A pilot is one approved change or one product family, a declared evidence population, a named owner on your side, and timing agreed in writing on day 0. It is a bounded way to see the Regulatory Change Integrity Review on your own records.

Scope

The records, devices or documents in scope are compared against the surfaces you declare: EUDAMED, UDI data, certificates, SS(C)P, labels, IFUs, declarations and controlled records. The size of the scope is agreed with you at the outset. Price is quoted after the scope brief.

How a pilot runs

Timing and scope are agreed in writing on day 0 and depend on how complete the evidence is. Timing pauses while evidence is outstanding, so a complete evidence pack keeps it short. The sequence below is the order of work, not a guaranteed schedule.

StageWhat happens
ScopeScope agreed in writing: approved source, surfaces, evidence route and your named regulatory owner.
IntakeControlled intake of the exports and records inside that scope.
ComparisonDeterministic comparison: source register, change-surface matrix and exception queue.
TriageTriage with your owner. Each exception is assigned; where an exception needs it, ClinicOps can involve independent specialists, by agreement with you.
DeliveryClosure packet delivered as a readable report and a machine-readable export.

What you receive

An exception queue, not a score. Each row carries source, expected, observed, status, evidence, owner, next action and closure. Statuses use this vocabulary: aligned, mismatch signal, missing evidence, conflicting evidence, unresolved, not applicable and requires qualified review.

See illustrative samples on fictional data in the specimen register (a simplified illustration) and the transition map sample. The full deliverable is described on the review page.

Who decides. ClinicOps compares regulated information and structures the review. Regulatory interpretation, materiality, safety significance and every final disposition stay with your qualified owner. A mismatch signal is not a compliance determination. ClinicOps is not a notified body or a competent authority.
