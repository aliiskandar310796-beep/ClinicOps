1 · Anchor the change

Evidence discipline

A useful comparison has three separate things:

  1. Reference — what your team approved.
  2. Observation — what each declared record currently contains.
  3. Decision — what a qualified owner does with the difference.

This tool keeps those layers separate on purpose.

2 · Map the propagation surface

Mark a record in scope only when your team expects it to reflect this change. An empty observation is treated as NOT OBSERVED, not as proof that the real record is missing.

What the completeness score means

The score is deliberately simple and inspectable. It rewards a declared reference value, reference evidence, in-scope observations, evidence references, and owners for unresolved items. It is an evidence-packet completeness measure only—not a risk score, regulatory grade or compliance score.
