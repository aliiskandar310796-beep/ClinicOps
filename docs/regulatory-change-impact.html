A regulation changed. Which of your documents does it touch?

Pick a category of recent EU regulatory change, answer six questions about a device portfolio, and get a deterministic impacted-surface checklist with severity bands plus an ordered evidence-review queue. Everything runs in this browser page — nothing you enter leaves it.

Method boundary. A sanitized demonstration of a working method, using generic change categories and fictional data. It is not regulatory monitoring, not legal or regulatory advice, and not a compliance determination; severity bands triage review effort and are not a regulatory-risk score. Confirm the specific change at the primary source (EUR-Lex, the Commission's medical-devices pages) and route decisions through your own QA/RA function.

This mapper structures the first hour after a regulatory change lands: which surfaces to look at, in what order, and what evidence each look should leave behind. When the queue exposes reconciliation across documents, unclear ownership or a register that no longer matches them, a scoped review takes it from there.
