Why the tools stay free

A screening signal is not the same thing as a reviewable decision. The free tools make low-cost signals visible. Paid work begins where evidence must be reconciled, source authority must be preserved, ambiguity must be routed to a human reviewer, or a team needs a work product that can survive internal review.
