Regulatory integrity review

Catch regulatory information drift before it becomes rework.

One regulated fact changes. How do you know every EUDAMED field, controlled record, IFU, SS(C)P, label or other declared surface that should change actually did?

ClinicOps Integrity Gate gives RA/QA teams a bounded, evidence-backed answer. It reconciles the sources your team declares as controlled, checks the downstream surfaces you put in scope, preserves source references, and routes ambiguity to a named human reviewer instead of guessing.

The failure mode

Source authority

If supplied controlled sources disagree, the case becomes UNRESOLVED_AUTHORITY. ClinicOps does not silently choose the winner.

Cross-surface reconciliation

Declared fields are compared only across the evidence population the client explicitly approves.

Change propagation

Approved old/new values can be checked against the downstream surfaces the client says are affected.

Human review

External ClinicOps outputs stay behind named review, finding dispositions and exact bundle hashes.

What a first review looks like

  • one device family or Basic UDI-DI group;
  • 1–3 client-approved controlled sources;
  • 2–6 downstream surfaces;
  • 3–10 declared fields;
  • optional approved change event with explicit old/new values;
  • one named qualified reviewer.

The output is a human-reviewed discrepancy and evidence pack with machine findings, source references, unresolved-authority cases, review minutes and reviewer dispositions.

Four fail-closed machine states

StateMeaning
PASSNo discrepancy found by the declared checks. Human review is still required before the ClinicOps work product is delivered.
REVIEW_REQUIREDA declared field is missing or differs from the single supplied controlled value.
UNRESOLVED_AUTHORITYThe supplied controlled evidence does not establish one authoritative value.
HOLD_FOR_HUMAN_DECISIONA declared change has not propagated, the approved source does not match the declared new value, or a downstream surface contains a third value.

Make the business case measurable

The review records current workload, automated checks executed, findings, reviewer minutes, confirmed findings, false positives and cases resolved only by human context. ClinicOps does not invent a generic savings percentage.

Boundary. Integrity Gate is an evidence-control and change-propagation workflow, not a legal, regulatory or compliance determination. Human approval clears only the exact ClinicOps output bundle for delivery; it never authorizes release of a device, controlled document or regulatory submission.
