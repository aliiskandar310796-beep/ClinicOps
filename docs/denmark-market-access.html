2. Danish clinical trial route map (medicinal products, CTR)

3. Device and IVD clinical investigation route

The Danish Medicines Agency describes an email route, outside EUDAMED, for device clinical investigations. Which authority gets the file depends on the article the investigation falls under. The Agency splits the route across eight sub-pages; the tables below join them. The EUDAMED transition page carries the module status.

Which route

Investigation typeWhat the authority page statesSource and page date
Article 62 investigationsMust be authorised by the Danish Medicines Agency before the investigation starts. Denmark applies a national rule that authorisation is required regardless of the class of the device.DKMA, notification duty, 3 May 2024
Article 74(1) investigations (PMCF with additional invasive or burdensome procedures)Assessed by the Medical Research Ethics Committees and notified to the Agency 30 days before the start. The Agency then has 30 calendar days to obtain more information. The page says the investigation is not subject to Agency authorisation. The ethics committees’ positive opinion is attached; the notification goes by email to dkma@dkma.dk.DKMA, notification duty, 3 May 2024; DKMA, PMCF notification, 3 May 2024
Article 82 investigations (other investigations)Submitted to the Medical Research Ethics Committees, which must issue a positive opinion. The page mentions no notification or authorisation to the Agency.DKMA, notification duty, 3 May 2024
When unsureA sponsor unsure whether an application is required is told to contact the Agency before applying.DKMA, clinical investigations, 3 May 2024
IVD performance studiesAn application to the Agency is required where sampling is surgically invasive only for the study, where results may influence patient management decisions or guide treatment, or where the study involves additional invasive procedures or other risks. For a CE-marked device used within its CE-marking with additional invasive or burdensome procedures, the Agency is notified 30 days before the start, by email with a short description. The page cites DS/ISO 20916:2019. It states no participant-information language, no SAE route and no fee amounts.DKMA, IVD performance studies, updated 16 July 2025

Danish text needs the same version control as everything else

ClinicOps treats translation and localisation as part of change control: approved source, target-language version, terminology decisions, reviewer identity, unresolved questions, approval and version closure. ClinicOps coordinates English↔Danish work, involves a specialist only for a defined question and with your approval, and never presents a reviewed translation as regulatory approval.

The same discipline covers pharmacovigilance. Global PV systems hit three Danish walls. The local medical journals that GVP Module VI (VI.B.1.1.2) expects marketing-authorisation holders to monitor are written in Danish. Danish-language case reports and narratives need medically literate handling inside statutory reporting timelines. And for DHPCs, the MAH supplies the Danish draft and the Danish Medicines Agency quality-assures it before distributing it. ClinicOps provides that support as controlled evidence work — source, target, terminology decision, reviewer, closure — while qualified PV sign-off remains with the client’s safety organisation. No QPPV, deputy or national contact-person role is taken unless explicitly appointed.

Where the Danish need actually sits

Denmark does not mandate a local PV contact person: the Danish Medicines Agency states it will generally not request one, and EMA’s member-state register records that it has never been required of any MAH. You are paying for competence, not a statutory placeholder.
