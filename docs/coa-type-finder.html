COA type finder and inventory

Most eCOA delays start with a question nobody answered early: who actually reports this, and what does that ask of the sites, the participants and the vendor? Answer four questions to sort an assessment into patient-, observer- or clinician-reported, or performance outcome. Then list every assessment in a study in one inventory and see what is missing before the specification is frozen. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent to ClinicOps.
