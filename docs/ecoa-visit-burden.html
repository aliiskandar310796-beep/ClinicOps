Visit and diary burden estimator

Participants answer questionnaires at visits and, in many studies, on a schedule at home. Enter how long each instrument takes, which visits and diaries use it, and see the minutes at each visit, in each study week and across the study. Set your own limits to find the visit or week that goes over. It runs in your browser; nothing is sent to ClinicOps.

Free tool

Boundary. This adds up the minutes and items you enter. It does not know how long any instrument takes, does not model fatigue, missed assessments or the time to travel to a site, and does not say what burden is acceptable. Take completion times from the instrument developer, a pilot or the vendor, and set limits from your protocol and ethics review. Do not enter participant data.

Nothing is stored between visits. Export the file to keep your work.

5. Result

What it leaves out

Time with the clinician, device set-up and training, reminders and late completions, and the effect of many assessments on dropout or data quality. Published figures for completion times exist for individual instruments, but they vary by population and device; use the developer's figure for your population and check it against a pilot where you can. The estimate also assumes every assessment is completed.
