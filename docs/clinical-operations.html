Evidence-controlled clinical operations

When clinical work changes, keep the evidence chain intact.

ClinicOps started with medical-device regulatory change integrity. The same operating problem appears in trials, quality systems and clinically used AI: one approved change touches many records, systems, vendors and owners, while a qualified human still has to decide what controls.

ClinicOps structures that work without turning machine findings into clinical, safety, legal or compliance conclusions.

Why these lanes are being tested now

Clinical trials

The European Commission states that from 31 January 2025 onward all clinical trials in the EU/EEA must be conducted under the Clinical Trials Regulation using CTIS. EMA lists ICH E6(R3) Principles and Annex 1 as effective in the EU from 23 July 2025.

Medical-device quality

FDA states that the Quality Management System Regulation became effective on 2 February 2026, amending 21 CFR part 820 and incorporating ISO 13485:2016 by reference; FDA also changed its device-inspection process on that date.

Clinical AI

The EU AI Act is in progressive application. Commission enforcement powers and Article 50 transparency obligations apply from 2 August 2026, while high-risk timelines vary by system category. ISO/IEC 42001:2023 provides an AI management-system framework that includes traceability, transparency and continual improvement.

How ClinicOps earns the right to expand

Discover → test → sell manually → prove repeatability → automate.

Each adjacent lane stays explicitly unvalidated until it has at least three qualified conversations, two independent confirmations of repeated pain and one concrete commercial signal such as a priced-scope request, proposed pilot, procurement step or identifiable budget owner. Productization waits for activated paid work and a repeatable human-review boundary.

That rule is intentional. ClinicOps will not build a software product merely because a regulation is large or a workflow can be automated.

What a ClinicOps packet preserves

Provenance

Which supplied/approved source supported each observation, with version or evidence references.

Uncertainty

Missing evidence, conflicting sources and unresolved authority stay visible instead of being converted into confident machine output.

Ownership

Every unresolved item routes to an accountable human owner; ClinicOps does not silently assume decision authority.

Boundary. These adjacent services are evidence-operations workflows, not medical advice, legal advice, clinical judgement, safety assessment, compliance certification, audit opinion, AI Act classification or release authorization. The qualified client/partner owner retains those decisions. Do not send patient-identifiable information through public ClinicOps pages.
