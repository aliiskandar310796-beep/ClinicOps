Clinical operations

Six clinical-operation niches, each with its own owner and evidence surface.

ClinicOps does not collapse trials, quality, AI, laboratories, clinics and medical content into one generic consulting offer. Each service has a different buyer, source set and reserved decision owner. The shared layer is evidence control and change integrity.

Scope a serviceAll services

TrialOps

For sponsors, CROs, sites and research partners managing protocol amendments, participant-facing changes, vendor/process changes, training and study-record propagation.

Entry service: Trial Change Integrity Review
Typical sources: protocol/amendment, CTIS/submission evidence, participant information, site communications, training, vendor/system records
Reserved owners: sponsor, investigator, ethics, regulatory and safety owners as applicable
Deliverable: change map, evidence register, unresolved queue, owner routing and closure packet
Denmark connection

Danish medicinal trials operate through the CTR/CTIS framework with the Danish Medicines Agency and research-ethics committees in the national assessment path.

Scope TrialOps

QualityOps

For medical-device quality teams and specialist partners controlling QMS, CAPA, SOP, supplier, training and controlled-record changes.

Entry service: QMS Change Integrity Review
Typical sources: approved change/CAPA, SOP/WI, forms, training, supplier records, configuration/master data, effectiveness evidence
Reserved owners: qualified QA/RA and process owners
Deliverable: affected-surface map, evidence gaps, review queue and closure packet
Use when

One approved quality change has multiple downstream surfaces and the team needs proof of implementation rather than another checklist.

Scope QualityOps

Clinical AI Operations

For healthcare and health-tech teams that need a reviewable evidence layer around clinically used or clinically relevant AI systems.

Entry service: Clinical AI Evidence Review
Typical sources: use-case description, vendor/system/version evidence, policies, change history, human-oversight records, unresolved questions
Reserved owners: clinical, safety, legal, regulatory, privacy and AI-governance owners as applicable
Deliverable: system/use-case register, provenance, ownership map, change register and evidence packet
Boundary

The service is not automatic AI Act classification, clinical validation, cybersecurity certification, privacy compliance or medical-device classification.

Scope Clinical AI

Lab & Diagnostic Operations

For laboratories, diagnostic services and point-of-care environments controlling changes to methods, equipment, documents, competencies and implementation evidence.

Entry service: Lab Change Integrity Review
Typical sources: method/version, equipment record, SOP, competency/training, QC/implementation evidence and owner approvals
Reserved owners: laboratory leadership and qualified clinical/quality owners
Deliverable: change evidence map and reviewer-ready closure record
Source discipline

Applicable laboratory standards and local regulatory sources are identified at scoping; ClinicOps does not assume one standard governs every lab workflow.

Scope LabOps

Clinic Operations

For clinics and care-delivery organisations controlling SOPs, vendor onboarding/renewal, staff acknowledgement/training and incident-to-action evidence.

Entry service: Clinic Operations Evidence Review
Typical sources: policies/SOPs, vendor evidence, staff roles/competencies, training, incidents, corrective actions and closure evidence
Reserved owners: clinical leadership, treatment-place management and other responsible healthcare owners
Deliverable: operational evidence register, unresolved queue and ownership map
Denmark connection

Where the work touches healthcare delivery, ClinicOps uses relevant Danish Patient Safety Authority sources and keeps treatment-provider responsibilities separate from consulting support.

Scope Clinic Operations

Controlled Medical Content & Localisation

For manufacturers, research teams, distributors and language/content partners that need approved-source content to remain controlled across versions, markets and languages.

Entry service: Controlled Medical Content Change Review
Typical sources: approved English/master content, target-language content, terminology decisions, labels/IFU/SS(C)P or study-facing materials, version history
Reserved owners: relevant medical, regulatory, quality, sponsor or content-approval owners
Deliverable: source-to-derivative map, terminology/version register, unresolved questions and approval evidence
Danish capability

English↔Danish localisation can be incorporated as a controlled evidence workflow rather than treated as an isolated translation task.
