Distribution is multi-channel

Direct

Bounded outreach and workflow comparisons where lawful and unsuppressed. Company-wide cold-email limits apply across every agent and every segment.

Partner

ARs, CROs, quality firms, clinical-technology vendors, laboratories and language/medical-content partners can carry ClinicOps evidence controls into existing client relationships.

Inbound

Browser-local tools, specimens, scope briefs and research let buyers inspect the method before sharing data.

Recurring

A one-off review expands only when the buyer confirms real cadence: amendments, QMS changes, vendor/model changes, expiries, document updates or repeated evidence handoffs.

Ready does not mean overclaimed

Operationally live: ClinicOps can scope and deliver a bounded human-reviewed engagement now.

Commercial proof: tracked separately. A live offer does not imply that market demand, compliance, safety, legal adequacy or a recurring product has already been proven.

Decision boundary. ClinicOps provides evidence control, reconciliation and change-operations support. Machine outputs are not clinical, legal, regulatory, safety, quality, compliance or release determinations. Client or partner decision owners retain those responsibilities.
