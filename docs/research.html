Evidence before interpretation.

ClinicOps research turns public records and supplied documents into reviewable evidence. ClinicOps notes where each fact came from, who holds which role, and what it could not confirm. It does not turn that into a pass or fail.

Secondary specialist work

Evidence-input boundary

If a public record is missing or two records disagree, ClinicOps flags it for a person to look at. It does not mean anyone did something wrong. In medical-device work the manufacturer, authorised-representative, importer and notified-body roles stay attached to the evidence; they are not flattened into generic document checking.

Primary sources

Regulatory and clinical statements are grounded in official public sources where they exist. Danish work starts with the Danish authority and connects to EU legislation, Commission and EMA systems and applicable guidance. Client-controlled evidence stays distinct from public-source evidence.

Citing, data and corrections

Cite as

ClinicOps, page title, review date shown on the page, https://clinicops.dk/…, accessed on your date. Cite the page version you read; pages carry a review date and are corrected in the open.

What is published

Aggregate counts and changes only. Named records, identifiers and document references are not published. From the next quarterly release no count below 10 will be shown; smaller counts will read “fewer than 10”.

What the numbers describe

The watchlist is private, so results describe the public records of that watchlist, not the register as a whole. When the watchlist changes, the release says so, because a change in the list changes the counts.

Wording

Where two records differ, the note says “records differ”. It does not say that anyone is non-compliant, and no firm is named.

Corrections so far

  • 8 September 2026. A class III headline was reversed: all 17 sampled MDR manufacturer registrations had linked SS(C)P metadata, so the finding is a legacy-to-MDR transition backlog, not a failure to document.
  • 9 September 2026. A deep-page reachability limit seen on 8 September did not reproduce on 9 September, and the method note was updated.
  • 12 September 2026. New Commission help material strengthened the evidence for the revised SS(C)P workflow but did not show that Production had started.
  • 14 November 2026. MDCG 2026-4 rev.1 moved Production deployment to December 2026 and the recommended legacy upload date to 27 March 2027; the affected pages were corrected.
  • 16 November 2026. Statements that implied a standing team, a staffed specialist network or an on-call pharmacovigilance resource were removed. ClinicOps is one person and approaches a specialist for one defined question, with the client’s approval.
  • 16 November 2026. The partners page said both that white-label work carries the partner’s name and that every deliverable carries a ClinicOps signature. It now separates a signed handover note from a white-label work product.
  • 16 November 2026. The description of ClinicOps as led from Denmark was replaced by “Denmark-specialised and EU-wide”, and a reference on the home page to limits stated elsewhere on the page, which did not exist, was replaced by the limits themselves.
  • 16 November 2026. The privacy notice no longer lists a person named in a vigilance role among outreach data, states which records it keeps instead of denying any list, and names AI assistants. The About page now says that ClinicOps is one person and covers AI-assisted drafting, not only the tools.

If you find an error, write to support@clinicops.dk with the page and the source. It is acknowledged within 2 working days and decided within 10 working days. A page under credible challenge carries a dated “under review” line until the question is decided. A correction is published with its date and logged in the open; a page is not quietly edited.

Research boundary. Public-source research is not a full audit of a manufacturer, trial, clinic or regulated system. Conclusions remain bounded by the declared evidence population and the qualified owner’s review.

Ask a question

You do not need to name your company, product or study. Describe the situation as a pattern and say what you want to know. Questions get a reply within three working days; the contact page has the template and the details.
