How the material is made

  • Each page names the official sources, the date they were read, and what is not established.
  • Pages are a ClinicOps synthesis of published guidance. They are not a standard, legal advice or a compliance determination.
  • Claims are registered with their read date. A correction is published with its date, not quietly edited (how corrections work).
  • Videos say when a voice is synthetic, and the studies they show are imagined, not clients.
  • Nothing here asks for patient data or confidential files.
