Expert network

Bring the right expertise into the workflow without blurring ownership.

ClinicOps can assemble project teams from a network of independent clinical researchers, life-science and MDR/clinical-trial consultants, Danish physicians, nurses and other relevant specialists. Participation is project-based and subject to credential verification, availability, conflict checks and a written scope.

Why the network exists

Clinical context

Bring practical healthcare or research context into evidence review without converting operational analysis into unsupported medical judgement.

Specialist depth

Add domain expertise for a specific product, study, quality system, language, clinical workflow or Danish-market question when the engagement requires it.

Resilient capacity

Scale delivery around real engagements without pretending every specialty is permanently staffed or automating decisions that require human expertise.

Governance before representation

Private credential registry

ClinicOps records only the professional information needed to assess fit: role, qualifications, authorisation status where applicable, relevant experience, language capability, availability, conflicts and permitted work types.

Project-specific appointment

Experts join only after scope, confidentiality, conflicts, deliverables, review authority, compensation and data access are agreed.

Accurate public profiles

Named profiles are published only with explicit consent and verified facts. Independent experts are not presented as employees, officers or standing clinical decision-makers unless that relationship actually exists.

Decision ownership

Every project states who advises, who reviews and who owns the final clinical, regulatory, quality, legal, safety or release decision.

Danish physicians and nurses

Danish healthcare professionals can support non-patient-facing consulting such as clinical-context review, evidence interpretation support, workflow design, medical-content review, research and scoped advisory work when their competence fits the task. Where regulated professional status matters, ClinicOps verifies it against the relevant official register.

KBU boundary. Danish medical authorisation and permission for independent medical practice are distinct statuses. Styrelsen for Patientsikkerhed states that a physician must have Danish medical authorisation and complete the clinical basis training (KBU) requirements before permission for independent practice can be issued. ClinicOps therefore never treats the title “doctor” or participation in KBU as automatic independent clinical decision authority.

Default operating boundary

The expert network is designed first for consulting and evidence work, not direct patient treatment. If an engagement ever crosses into healthcare delivery, patient-specific medical activity or treatment-place operations, ClinicOps requires a separate governance review covering the relevant provider, supervision, authorisation, insurance, data-protection and documentation obligations before work starts.

Independent does not just mean “send an invoice”

Danish tax treatment depends on the actual working relationship. A consultant can be treated as an employee, fee recipient or independent business depending on the facts; the label in a contract or invoice does not decide the classification. Repeated or economically dependent engagements are therefore reviewed before assignment.

No borrowed authority. ClinicOps does not use a clinician’s title to make a regulatory, legal or compliance conclusion look stronger than the evidence supports. Expertise is attached only to the task actually reviewed.

For prospective experts

Experts who want to be available to ClinicOps can register their professional profile privately. Registration does not guarantee work and does not create employment or public representation by itself.
