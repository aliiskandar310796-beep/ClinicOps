Working with partners: narrow units, one accountable owner.

ClinicOps takes bounded units of work from regulatory consultancies, authorised representatives and sponsors who need a second pair of hands on records and language, and brings in outside specialists only for a defined question. It does this through a private, project-specific expert-network model, not a standing team.

Units a partner can hand over

Identifier and certificate reconciliation

Compare a list of devices, UDI-DIs and certificates against the public EUDAMED records and your own master data. You get an exception queue and a closure packet. Records that differ are listed as differences to resolve, not as findings about compliance.

Pre-submission completeness read

A read of a submission pack or a change package against a checklist you supply or approve: what is missing, inconsistent between documents, or not yet decided. It does not assess whether the content meets a requirement.

Native Danish review

Danish-language review of clinical and device text against the approved source, with open questions routed to the accountable owner. For medicines safety communications, see the DHPC checker.

Rules that do not bend

  • No silent subcontracting. If anyone other than ClinicOps will touch your material, you are told who does what before work starts, and you can say no.
  • NDA first. Confidential material is exchanged only after a signed confidentiality agreement. Do not send patient or personal data.
  • One accountable owner. The ClinicOps engagement lead signs every deliverable, even when a specialist contributed. Specialists are not represented as ClinicOps employees, officers, notified bodies, competent authorities or decision owners.
  • Your name stays yours. White-label work is delivered to you in your format for you to put your name on, if you agree this in writing. ClinicOps does not contact your clients.
  • Inbound only. ClinicOps does not cold-contact organisations to sell partner work.

A CV, job title or language skill is not treated as proof of fit. The assignment has to match the person’s verified work type, jurisdiction, language and professional boundary. Availability, confidentiality, commercial terms and any professional-authorisation requirements are checked before activation. The roster is private: contact details, rates, credential documents and contracts stay in an access-controlled system, and a named public profile appears only with consent and verified wording.

Professional boundary. Legal, regulatory, clinical, safety, quality and release decisions remain with the explicitly accountable person or organisation. A specialist’s observation becomes evidence in the review; it does not automatically become your decision. The network is a controlled delivery model, not a claim that any specialty or country is permanently staffed.

Sample scope (fictional)

Example only, with invented data. A real scope is agreed in writing before work starts.

Unit

Identifier reconciliation for a fictional portfolio of 120 device records.

You provide

A CSV with device name, UDI-DI, Basic UDI-DI, certificate number and manufacturer reference; the date you want the work finished; the person who will decide each exception.

You receive

An exception queue (each row: the two records that differ, the field, the source of each), a closure packet showing what was compared and what was not, and a short list of open questions for your decision owner.

Accepted when

Every input row appears in the output once; every exception names both sources; nothing is labelled compliant or non-compliant; questions are phrased so that they can be answered yes, no or “not known”.

Stops at

Delivery of the packet. Changes to the scope go in writing; corrections to your own records are yours to make. Files are handed over on exit.

Not included

Opinion on whether any device meets a requirement, contact with notified bodies or authorities, authorised-representative duties, and anything outside the CSV.
