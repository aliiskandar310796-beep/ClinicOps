Rules as of 16 November 2026 · read at the source; registry CO-CLM-0021: CTR Arts. 5-8: initial application periods in CTIS.

Free tool · durations from Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 Articles 5 to 8

Short answer

Under Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 Articles 5 to 8, validation of an initial clinical trial application takes 10 days (up to 25 with a request for information), Part I takes 45 days and Part II takes 45 days, and the decision follows within 5 days of the later of the Part I reporting date and the end of Part II. Without requests for information the periods give 60 days; with all of them, the EMA CTIS Sponsor Handbook states 106.

Source: Regulation (EU) No 536/2014, Articles 5 to 8; Regulation (EU) No 536/2014 as consolidated to 5 December 2022 (OJ); EMA CTIS Sponsor Handbook section 3.1 (7 July 2026). Read at the source on 16 November 2026. Registry CO-CLM-0021.

How the dates are worked out
  • Day 0 is the submission date. Periods are calendar days; a task starts the day after the previous one ends.
  • Validation: 10 days. With a request for information, the sponsor has up to 10 days to respond and the reporting Member State 5 more, so validation is notified by Day 25 at the latest.
  • Part I: 45 days from the validation date, in three steps of 26, 12 and 7 days. A request for information adds up to 31 days (12 for the sponsor, 12 for review, 7 for consolidation). The advanced-therapy extension adds up to 50.
  • Part II: 45 days from the validation date, in parallel with Part I. A request for information adds up to 31 days (12 for the sponsor, 19 for the Member State).
  • Decision: 5 days after the later of the Part I reporting date and the end of Part II. Without requests, the periods give 60 days; with all of them, 106.
  • Weekends: a due date on a Saturday, Sunday or a date you list moves to the next working day, and every later step starts from the moved date.
  • Winter stop: the EMA handbook describes a pause from 22 December 23:59 to 8 January, so 23 December to 7 January do not count. It is an EMA and CTCG practice, not in the Regulation.
  • Where a request falls: this tool places each request for information at the end of the assessment it extends. The Regulation gives the maximum extension, not the day the request is issued.

Sources: Regulation (EU) No 536/2014; EMA CTIS Sponsor Handbook, section 3.1 (7 July 2026); Regulation No 1182/71, Article 3.
