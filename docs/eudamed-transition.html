Why this is time-sensitive

28 November 2026 per the Commission's legacy-device transition guidance (notice published 27 November 2025; mandatory use 28 May 2026; 6-month transition). Decision (EU) 2025/2371 itself states no calendar date. 28 November 2026 is a Saturday: plan to finish by Friday 27 November 2026. Whether a device counts as a legacy device is the manufacturer's determination, so check the Commission documents and the Regulation for your devices.

What the review can organise

  • manufacturer and actor-role context;
  • legacy versus MDR registration path;
  • identifier reconciliation questions;
  • certificate or transition timing where reliable evidence is supplied;
  • SS(C)P workflow questions where applicable;
  • unresolved evidence gaps;
  • a prioritised human-review queue.

Legacy identifier screening

For legacy devices, the EUDAMED DI carries the B- prefix and the Commission help pages describe it as the equivalent of the Basic UDI-DI. The prefix is a screening input: it does not by itself establish a device's regulatory or SS(C)P state, and ClinicOps never treats it as a compliance conclusion.

Designed for portfolio operations

The Transition Map earns its place when the problem is portfolio-scale reconciliation, not a single record: authorised representatives managing several manufacturers, manufacturers with class III or implantable transition workload, and consultancies that want a repeatable evidence layer.

Deliverable

A pilot returns intake diagnostics, role-aware portfolio segmentation, a priority work queue, an evidence-gap ledger and a human-reviewed action plan: a work plan, not an allegation that any manufacturer is non-compliant.
