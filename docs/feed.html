Follow ClinicOps updates by feed or calendar.

The feed lists the claims in the ClinicOps registry that have been read at source, plus the dated corrections log. It is made for a feed reader, so opening the raw file in a browser shows code, not a page.

Add the feed to a reader

Copy this address and paste it into a feed reader such as Feedly, Inoreader, NetNewsWire or Thunderbird:

https://clinicops.dk/feed.xml

Each entry names the registry id, the page it applies to and the source that was read. Check the source for your own devices or trials; the entries are not legal advice.

Add the calendar

Download regulatory-dates.ics and open it in a calendar app, or subscribe by address:

https://clinicops.dk/regulatory-dates.ics

The calendar holds the dated items ClinicOps tracks, with the plan date where the published date falls on a weekend.

Prefer to read it here

The current evidence and corrections are on the research page. There is no email newsletter, no account and no tracking behind the feed or the calendar.
