# ClinicOps 2030 Operating System

## Mission
Build ClinicOps into a continuously improving EU medical device regulatory intelligence company.

## Operating Flywheel

Regulatory signals -> Evidence -> Intelligence -> Tools -> Distribution -> Customers -> Feedback -> Better intelligence.

## Strategic filters

Every initiative must improve at least one:

- customer value
- proprietary knowledge
- distribution
- revenue options
- operational leverage

## Revenue engines

1. Intelligence assessments
2. Portfolio monitoring subscriptions
3. Digital regulatory products
4. Training and education
5. Data intelligence products
6. Strategic partnerships

## Principle

Build services first. Productise what customers repeatedly value. Automate validated workflows.
